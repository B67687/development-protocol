## The thesis (user's, preserved)

Naive creativity made with heart beats soulless polished AI work (Niu Lai lesson;
early-Roblox endearment). Goal: understand what "this" is so agents can do it —
**the strength of naivety + the strength of systems** — possibly integrated with
the dev protocol. Long-vision, user asked how to start.

## Definition (session synthesis)

**Naive creativity = Heart + Constraint + Ignorance of Conventions → Authentic Expression.**

Properties: (1) imperfection as feature — cracks signal human presence;
(2) heart/stake — creator cares about something specific, not about "being good";
(3) constraint-driven — limited skill forces creative solutions;
(4) authentic voice — expression over professionalism;
(5) convention blindness — breaking unknown rules produces surprise;
(6) emotional honesty — reveals genuine obsessions.
Prioritizes _expression_ over _execution_.

## Research anchors (from librarian sub-session; redo only to challenge these)

1. **Doshi & Hauser 2024, Science (1,338 cites):** generative AI enhances individual
   creativity but **reduces collective diversity** — output converges to median.
   "Polished mediocrity" is documented.
2. **Buehler (MIT, 2025), selective imperfection:** novelty emerges when constraints
   can't be satisfied in existing degrees of freedom; culturally significant systems
   cluster in a **mid-entropy, mid-defect corridor**. Too perfect = boring, too
   random = noise. Sweet spot = structured imperfection.
3. **Liu et al. 2024 (ACM CSCW), "Smart Error":** designers prefer certain AI errors
   as inspiration ("atomic inspiration"); imperfect AI > perfect AI for ideation.
4. **Constrained creativity** as growing theme (Hu et al. 2025, Magnani 2025/2026,
   Sabbani); **interestingness evaluation** (Bae & Kim 2024, Ismayilzada et al. 2024,
   Artiles NeurIPS 2026 Cultural Alien Sampler); **wabi-sabi / aesthetic aphasia**
   (Lin & Kuo 2026); hallucination-as-creativity; machine neuroaesthetics (Hu).

## The AI problem (core tension)

Agents optimize correctness/completeness/convention-adherence ("professional" output).
Naive creativity requires LIMITATION + HEART + IGNORANCE. AI has INFINITE CAPACITY +
NO HEART + TOTAL CONVENTION KNOWLEDGE. Exact opposite.

## World-model insight (user's reframing, kept)

Naivety may be **emergent from friction with reality**, not engineerable into LLMs:
LLMs generate from _distribution_; embodied systems generate from _friction_.
Consequence: LLM work = constraint injection + convention blindness + personas with
genuine preferences + interestingness evaluation (achievable); genuine surprise,
authentic imperfection, and heart stay out of reach until world interaction arrives.
