## 1. Executive Summary

**Key finding:** the literature converges on one load-bearing split and the thought's phrasing gets it right. Governance (who decides what good data *is* — decision rights, principles, categories) is separable from management (the machinery that implements it), and the founding framework of the field defines governance as exactly five decision domains, two of which — data quality and metadata — are pure method content **[LIKELY]**. The reusable core the method half should import is FAIR: persistent identifiers, rich metadata, registered/indexed, standard protocols, shared vocabularies, qualified references, license + provenance + community standards — with the primary paper explicitly stating the principles *precede implementation* and are *not a standard* **[CONFIRMED]**. The contested/weak points: quantified ROI of governance rests on vendor-cited figures whose primaries were not verifiable here (Gartner $12.9M/yr, Eckerson $600B/yr **[UNVERIFIED]**); provenance helps trust but is insufficient alone — users also demand quality metrics, the data itself, and author reputation **[LIKELY]**; and the governance literature itself is defining-heavy and implementing/monitoring-thin (31 papers → 110 activities, "lack of reported" implementing/monitoring action **[UNVERIFIED — record-level]**).

## 2. Governance vs Management: Where the Method Half Lives

**Rule the evidence supports: the spec owns decision rights (what counts as governed data, who is accountable, which categories exist); the engine owns implementation (retrieval, extraction, indexing).** This is not an analogy — it is the field's founding distinction.

- **Khatri & Brown's framework (the locus classicus).** "Governance refers to what decisions must be made to ensure effective management and use of IT (*decision domains*) and who makes the decisions (*locus of accountability*)… Management involves making and implementing decisions. For example, governance includes establishing who holds decision rights for determining standards for data quality. Management involves determining the actual metrics employed." Five data decision domains: data principles, data quality, metadata, data access, data lifecycle **[LIKELY — peer-reviewed CACM abstract extracted via SearXNG (https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1629175.1629210) + converging community summary (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_governance)]**. Design implication for the T-069 split: *data principles, data quality standards, and metadata* are the three domains that live in SPECIFICATION.md/STANDING_PRINCIPLES; *data access and data lifecycle* mechanics live closer to the engine. The thought's "method vs engine" cut falls within one domain of the field's own cut.
- **The field under-implements.** A literature analysis (six IS databases, 31 papers, open coding → 110 governance activities across the five domains) finds "a high volume of activities associated with the *defining* action… with a lack of reported [implementing] and monitoring actions" **[UNVERIFIED — peer-reviewed record, snippet-level (https://doi.org/10.1080/12460125.2016.1187397)]**. Design implication: a method half that only *defines* categories repeats the field's own failure mode. Every governed-data rule in the spec needs an implementing check (lint/ledger, machine-checked) and a monitoring cadence — the same lesson as the thought-log gate (machine-checked, not advisory).
- **Drivers are compliance-first, quality-second.** A 2017 practitioner survey (CIO WaterCooler, n unspecified): 54% process efficiency, 39% regulatory, 7% customer service as the key driver **[UNVERIFIED — community-cited, weak source (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_governance)]**. For protocol purposes: do not justify data-governance rules by compliance (no regulator here); justify by the quality mechanism (§3–§5) or drop the rule.
- **Local grounding.** SPECIFICATION §1.6 source manifest (T-030: the spec names its sources) and the readings-gate enumerate+pin+confirm rule are already method-side governance of *input* data — LOCAL-CONFIRMED. What is missing is the symmetric treatment of *analysis* data: intermediate results, cached extractions, and ledger entries have no stated provenance/categorisation rule.

## 3. Quality Dimensions & Fitness-for-Use

**Rule the evidence supports: define quality as fitness-for-use against named dimensions, and scope every DQ check so it does not duplicate business logic.**

- **The dimension list is stable; the measures are not.** Canonical dimensions recur across sources: accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, validity, uniqueness (governance-team sextet), plus accessibility, credibility/reliability, relevance, comparability, flexibility, plausibility **[LIKELY — community consensus (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_quality)]**. But "nearly 200 such terms have been identified and there is little agreement in their nature… definitions or measures (Wang et al., 1993)", and a systematic scoping review finds dimensions and methods "not consistent in the literature" for real-world data **[LIKELY — community + cited peer-reviewed lineage]**. Design implication: the spec should *fix a small closed dimension set* (six is the governance-team norm) rather than inherit the literature's 200-term sprawl. Unbounded dimension lists are the categorisation analogue of unbounded gates.
- **Fitness-for-use is the definition that survives.** Consumer ("fit for use by data consumers"), business ("fit for intended uses in operations, decision making and planning"), and standards-based ("degree to which inherent characteristics fulfil requirements") perspectives converge on comparing actual state to a *desired state defined by the consumer* **[LIKELY — community consensus (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_quality)]**. Design implication: every dataset the method governs needs a named consumer and intended use; data with no consumer is PARKED or DROPPED, never left OPEN — the same commitment precondition as the disposition machine.
- **Cost-of-poor-quality numbers motivate but do not calibrate.** Gartner $12.9M/yr average; Eckerson $600B/yr to the US economy (2002); USPS/PwC 23.6% of US mail incorrectly addressed; 45M+ Americans change address yearly **[UNVERIFIED — all secondary/vendor-cited; primaries not extracted]**. Encode as motivation ("quality failures are expensive"), never as quantified targets or ROI promises.
- **DQ scope discipline (directly importable).** "DQ checks and business rules may easily overlap… Data quality checks are redundant if business logic covers the same functionality"; DQ scope belongs in a DQ strategy; checks sit at attribute level, at point-of-entry for mandatory attributes, validity checks against reference data, accuracy checks against third-party sources after multi-hop transit, consistency checks for master-data columns **[LIKELY — practitioner consensus, community source (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_quality)]**. The spec analogue: EXTRACTION/REVIEW gates are business logic; data-governance checks are the *preconditions on inputs* (source named? license stated? freshness dated? provenance linked?). If a REVIEW row already covers it, a second DQ check is overlap, not depth.

## 4. FAIR as the Method-Side Contract

**Rule the evidence supports: require Findable/Accessible/Interoperable/Reusable handling for every dataset the method touches, applied incrementally — FAIR is a pre-implementation guide, not a tooling mandate.**

- **The principles (primary-source extraction).** Wilkinson et al., *Scientific Data* 2016 (Lorentz/FORCE11 lineage): F1 globally-unique persistent identifiers; F2 rich metadata; F3 metadata carries the data identifier; F4 registered/indexed in a searchable resource; A1 retrievable by identifier over standard protocol (A1.1 open/free/universal; A1.2 auth where needed); A2 metadata outlives data; I1 formal shared knowledge-representation language; I2 FAIR vocabularies; I3 qualified cross-references; R1 rich attributes; R1.1 clear license; R1.2 detailed provenance; R1.3 community standards **[CONFIRMED — full-text extraction (https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18) + converging community articulation (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAIR_data)]**.
- **Three load-bearing sentences from the primary (verbatim, extracted):** (a) "good data management and stewardship is not a goal in itself, but rather a pre-condition supporting knowledge discovery and innovation"; (b) "These high-level FAIR Guiding Principles precede implementation choices, and do not suggest any specific technology, standard, or implementation-solution; moreover, the Principles are not, themselves, a standard or a specification"; (c) "The Principles may be adhered to in any combination and incrementally, as data providers' publishing environments evolve to increasing degrees of 'FAIRness'" **[CONFIRMED — extracted primary text]**. For the T-069 split, (b) is the charter: the method half states FAIR-shaped requirements; the engine half chooses tooling. A method rule that names a tool has crossed the split.
- **Non-data assets included.** "Analytical workflows… are a critical component… The FAIR principles can equally be applied to these non-data assets, which need to be identified, described, discovered, and reused in much the same manner as data" **[CONFIRMED — extracted primary text]**. Design implication: analysis procedures (LANDSCAPE strategies, synthesis templates, ledger schemas) are governed data too — identified, versioned, referenced.
- **The sensitive-data pattern (directly importable).** Rich metadata publication "provides a high degree of 'FAIRness' even in the absence of FAIR publication of the data itself"; Dataverse keeps "metadata always public, even if the data are restricted or removed" **[CONFIRMED — extracted primary text]**. Protocol analogue: session-local or paywalled material gets a metadata record (what it is, where it lives, access path, e.g. the A7 school-library route) even when content cannot be quoted — RECALLED stays RECALLED, but *findable*.
- **Maturity machinery exists; adopt the ladder, not the grade.** RDA FAIR Data Maturity Model (2020): 41 indicators across the principles; JRC FAIR Data Guidelines (2025): five progressive levels (start → play → go → share → "FAIRest"), explicitly "not a grading scale" and "not all datasets need to strive for the highest maturity level"; FAIRness decays without maintenance; guides put a FAIR data-management plan at ~5% of research budget **[LIKELY — community + cited institutional sources, no independent extraction (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAIR_data)]**. Institutional uptake (G20 Hangzhou 2016 endorsement; Horizon Europe FAIR-by-default; Sorbonne 2020 declaration; Scheffler et al. *Nature* 2022 "a must" for AI/data mining) shows the contract is externally legible — method rules phrased in FAIR terms interoperate with funders and publishers **[LIKELY — same basis]**.
- **Known failure mode: conceptual dilution.** "Awareness of the FAIR concept was increasing… but understanding was becoming confused as different people apply their own differing perspectives" (2017) **[LIKELY — community-cited peer-reviewed lineage]**. Guard: the spec must pin its FAIR subset explicitly (which sub-principles, at which maturity level) rather than saying "be FAIR". Complement: CARE Principles (Collective benefit, Authority, Responsibility, Ethics, GIDA 2019) extend FAIR where power/history differentials apply — out of scope for single-stakeholder protocol use, noted so a future multi-user fold does not reinvent it **[LIKELY — community basis]**.

## 5. Provenance, Lineage & Trust

**Rule the evidence supports: record provenance for every analysis input, but treat provenance as necessary-not-sufficient — ship it with quality metrics, the data (or its metadata record), and source reputation.**

- **The trust study (extracted).** Donaldson & Fear, TaPP 2011 (USENIX): semi-structured interviews with 17 proteomics researchers using ProteomeCommons.org. "Provenance does help end-users gauge the trustworthiness of data and build their confidence in reusing data. However, provenance also needs to be accompanied by other kinds of information, including: more specific data quality information, the data itself, and author reputation information" **[LIKELY — full record + abstract extracted via sidecar (http://hdl.handle.net/2022/22474); single qualitative study, no second source]**. Design implication: a provenance link alone does not discharge the trust requirement. The synthesis template's per-claim quartet should be *source + provenance + quality signal + reputation/confidence* — provenance is one leg of four.
- **Pre-requirements traceability is the same structure.** The thought-log sweep's §5 finding stands cross-referenced here, not re-researched: T-entries are stakeholder utterances; analysis inputs are the same class of object. Fold-without-backlink is post-RS-only traceability in both cases (see `thought-log-handling-2026-10-03.md` §5).
- **Lineage can be cheap; budget it anyway.** Healthcare Integration Lineage Instrumentation Framework (HILIF, W3C-PROV-grounded, sidecar ring-buffer, dual relational+graph store): 120 channels / 14 stages / 450 msg-s over 72h → mean overhead 3.2 ms/msg, sustained throughput −1.5%, capture completeness 99.9999%, forward-trace <200 ms median **[UNVERIFIED — single-paper snippet (https://doi.org/10.63282/3050-9262.ijaidsml-v7i1p159)]**. Usable as an existence proof that provenance capture need not be a tax — but the protocol analogue (session-close link repair) must still be *budgeted*, because the traceability literature says link upkeep is the dominant cost.
- **Automated pre-pipeline gates move measured quality.** A domain-agnostic DQ assessment/remediation framework (completeness, uniqueness, consistency, accuracy; Pandas/NumPy) on 10,234 multi-source e-commerce records: 15,540 structural issues found (above prior manual validation), overall quality 97.46% → 99.11%, validation time −95%; authors' conclusion: "pre-production data governance should be treated as foundational AI infrastructure rather than an optional preprocessing step" **[UNVERIFIED — preprint snippet only (https://www.ssrn.com/abstract=7038958); preprint cap = LIKELY max, snippet = UNVERIFIED]**. Directionally supports gate-before-analysis (§6); do not encode the percentages.

## 6. Categorisation, Catalogs & Quality Gates

**Rule the evidence supports: categorise once in a governed catalog (metadata-first), reference everywhere else; push checks to point-of-entry; keep governance machine-checked.**

- **Metadata is the mechanism.** The FAIR metadata-stewardship synthesis: "metadata as the primary mechanism through which FAIRness is operationalized… metadata-centered design enables machine-actionable discovery, integration, and reuse" **[LIKELY — community + preprint-report basis (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20027073)]**. A 2021 study identifies FAIR principles as a conceptual component of data-catalog software, alongside metadata management, business context, and responsibility roles **[UNVERIFIED — community-cited (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAIR_data)]**. Protocol analogue: the existing INDEX.md row-per-artifact pattern *is* a catalog; extend the pattern — every governed dataset gets one metadata record (identifier, source, license, freshness date, quality dimensions asserted, provenance link), referenced by consumers, never re-described inline.
- **Categories must be closed and owned.** The 200-term dimension sprawl (§3) and the FAIR-dilution warning (§4) both punish open vocabularies. The spec should fix: (a) a closed data-category list (e.g. source-evidence / analysis-intermediate / ledger-record / external-reference / sensitive-metadata-only); (b) one owner per category definition; (c) a change process (LANDSCAPE-first, like any fold). Practitioner convergence only: data contracts (Open Data Contract Standard — ODCS; Jones' *Driving Data Quality with Data Contracts*; data-mesh "shift-left" quality to producers; real-time Kafka contract enforcement at Grab) independently converged on schema+semantics+SLA declared at the producer boundary **[UNVERIFIED — practitioner literature, no peer-review claim (https://www.datamesh-manager.com/learn/what-is-a-data-contract; https://engineering.grab.com/real-time-data-quality-monitoring)]**. Import the shape (declare-at-entry, lint the declaration), not the tooling.
- **Gate placement: entry, not exit.** DQ checks belong "at the point of entry for each mandatory attribute from each source system"; validity against reference data; accuracy against third-party sources after multi-hop transit; timeliness/leeway against SLA to bound value decay **[LIKELY — practitioner consensus (§3 basis)]**. Protocol mapping: INBOX/EXTRACTION entry gates check *inputs* (source pinned? license noted? freshness dated?); REVIEW checks *outputs*. A quality gate that only inspects the final artifact is exit-testing — the configuration manufacturing abandoned.
- **Monitoring is the missing half.** Beyond the literature's implementing/monitoring gap (§2), data observability practice (freshness/volume/schema-drift monitors, "data downtime" framing) treats post-entry drift as first-class **[UNVERIFIED — practitioner framing]**. Minimum viable monitoring for the method half: freshness dates on every governed record + a stale-record sweep at session close (mirrors parked-without-trigger lint).

## 7. Anti-Patterns

| # | Anti-pattern | What it looks like | Why the evidence forbids it |

| 1 | **Governance-as-decoration** | Principles written, no implementing check, no monitoring | The field's own failure mode: defining-heavy, implementing/monitoring-thin literature **[UNVERIFIED]** (§2); document-only conditions underperform enforced ones (cf. experimenter-led > document-only pattern) |
| 2 | **Tool-in-the-method** | Spec mandates a catalog/contract tool instead of requirements | FAIR "precede implementation… not, themselves, a standard or specification" **[CONFIRMED]** (§4) |
| 3 | **Open vocabulary** | Ad-hoc category names per artifact; 200-term sprawl | Dimension-measure disagreement (Wang et al. 1993 lineage) **[LIKELY]** (§3); FAIR conceptual dilution **[LIKELY]** (§4) |
| 4 | **Provenance-only trust** | Link recorded, no quality signal, no confidence | Provenance needs DQ info + data + reputation alongside **[LIKELY]** (§5) |
| 5 | **Exit-only gating** | Quality checked solely at final REVIEW | Entry-gate placement consensus **[LIKELY]** (§3, §6); pre-production governance as infrastructure **[UNVERIFIED]** (§5) |
| 6 | **Data without a consumer** | Governed records with no named use | Fitness-for-use definition **[LIKELY]** (§3); commitment precondition (§2) |
| 7 | **Inline re-description** | Each artifact re-describes its sources | Catalog-once/reference pattern; metadata-as-mechanism **[LIKELY]** (§6) |
| 8 | **Unbudgeted links** | Backlinks assumed free, rot unmonitored | Link upkeep is the dominant traceability cost (cross-ref thought-log §5) |
| 9 | **Citing cost figures as targets** | "$12.9M saved" in a spec | Primaries unverified; motivation ≠ calibration **[UNVERIFIED]** (§3) |

**Open gaps (do not encode beyond these):**

1. **No direct literature on spec-governed analysis data.** Everything above is imported from enterprise/scientific data governance; no paper validates FAIR-shaped rules inside an agent-protocol spec. Encode candidate principles as *derived design*, not science.
2. **Cost/ROI uncalibrated.** All cost figures are UNVERIFIED; the method half has no measured defect rate of its own. A future ledger (scored predictions, cf. T-068) could supply the first protocol-native numbers.

Institutional-access retrieval (A7 school-library path) still owed for Abraham-style records and the SSRN preprint.

**Implementation notes (for the SPECIFICATION.md / STANDING_PRINCIPLES fold — derived, not encoded here):** candidate method-side rules the evidence supports — (a) *name the consumer*: every governed dataset states intended use (fitness-for-use, §3); (b) *catalog once*: one metadata record per dataset (identifier, source, license, freshness, dimensions asserted, provenance link), referenced not re-described (§6); (c) *gate at entry*: INBOX/EXTRACTION input preconditions, REVIEW stays output-scoped (§6); (d) *provenance quartet*: source + provenance + quality signal + confidence on every load-bearing claim (§5); (e) *metadata-only fallback*: unquotable material still gets a findable record (§4); (f) *closed categories + machine checks*: fixed category list, freshness lint at session close (§6); (g) *no tool names, no cost figures as targets* in method text (§4, §3). Suggested principle shape: "the spec governs data by decision rights, not machinery."
