## 3. Top Findings (with confidence + effect sizes)

**F1. Over half of real open-domain questions are ambiguous in four structural kinds the gate does not enumerate. (CONFIRMED, extractor full-verified today)**
AmbigQA/AmbigNQ (http://arxiv.org/abs/2004.10645v2): 14,042 NQ-OPEN questions, *over half* ambiguous — event references 39%, properties 27%, entity references 23%, answer types 16%. None is a "term with 2+ readings": they are *which-event / which-entity / which-counting-rule / which-question* ambiguities. A user asking "how many episodes in season 2?" carries no flagged term and sails through with a silently assumed counting rule. The gate needs an event/entity/counting-rule trigger alongside its term trigger.

**F2. Models default to answering, not clarifying — selective clarification beats both always-ask and never-ask. (LIKELY, extractor full-verified today)**
CLAM (http://arxiv.org/abs/2212.07769v2): "current language models rarely ask users to clarify ambiguous questions and instead provide incorrect answers"; pipeline = detect ambiguity → generate clarifying question → answer after clarification, with simulated users holding privileged information; "significantly improves accuracy on mixed ambiguous and unambiguous questions." Corroboration: Tree of Clarifications recursively disambiguates via few-shot + external knowledge and tops ASQA few-shot on all metrics (https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.63, snippet); CLARINET (retrieval setting) beats information-gain heuristics by 17% and vanilla-prompted LLMs by 39% relative (http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15784v1, snippet → numbers LIKELY max). Two transfers: (i) the stakes test is the right selectivity criterion — independent corroboration of *selective* over *universal* clarification; (ii) CLAM's simulated-user method is the ready-made gate test harness (gate-vs-no-gate, privileged-information roleplay).

**F3. Scope ambiguity is invisible to term enumeration. (LIKELY, convergent snippets)**
"Every hiker climbed a hill" — same-hill vs different-hill; competent producers *avoid* ambiguous negative-quantified forms, so the form itself signals likely-unintentional ambiguity (https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327817la1302_5). The priming record is contested in a useful way: Chemla & Bott scope-priming claim vs confound-replication finding priming disappears when the confound is removed (https://www.glossa-journal.org/article/id/5283/, snippet) — representations are similarity-sensitive, not abstract-inversion operations. Design consequence stands either way: scope pins must be concrete ("who did what to whom, same-X or each-own-X?"), never generic ("check quantifiers").

**F4. Anaphora tooling exists for pronouns — bridging and discourse deixis remain the gap. (LIKELY; tool-existence CONFIRMED, numbers snippet)**
TAPHSIR (http://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10227v1 — extractor full-verified today): hybrid ML detector + BERT-variant resolver; per-pronoun ambiguous/unambiguous verdict plus automatic interpretation, output "easily reviewed and validated by requirements engineers." What TAPHSIR does *not* cover is the T-007 remainder: (a) *bridging* — "deploy the service; the port is already open" (never introduced); CODI-CRAC 2021 ran a dedicated bridging/discourse-deixis shared task precisely because standard coreference misses it; (b) *discourse deixis* — "this is too risky" binds a whole plan, not a noun phrase. Honest calibration for any automated flag stays recall-100%/precision-~60% (RE prior, snippet): over-flag, human discards.

**F5. Vagueness is not ambiguity; only nocuous ambiguity deserves handling. (LIKELY, theoretical convergence)**
Ambiguity offers enumerable readings; vagueness (*fast, scalable, robust, done*) offers borderline cases with no readings to list — enumeration, confirmation-pick, and discarded-readings logging all assume a discrete candidate set that does not exist. The nocuous-ambiguity programme extends from coordination to anaphora (http://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5636921/, snippet/paywalled → LIKELY). The stakes test already approximates the nocuous line; vagueness needs its own instrument — a *threshold probe* ("what concrete case would fail this?"), not a candidate list.

**F6. Pragmatic ambiguity is detectable via multi-expertise discrepancy simulation. (LIKELY, extractor full-verified today — new-vs-10-03 delta)**
RAG pragmatic-ambiguity framework (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04436 — extractor full-verified today): novice/intermediate/expert knowledge bases simulate stakeholders, discrepancies flag pragmatic ambiguity; GPT-4o-mini recall 0.75/F2 0.75 (snippet-level numbers → LIKELY max, never design rationale). Reusable gate test: would a novice and an expert read this requirement differently? New rule owed: definite-without-introduction + factive/recurrence triggers ("again", "still", "stop", "continue") get a presupposition flag; directive-form questions ("can/could/would you…") get a force check.

**F7. Attachment ambiguity + lingering misinterpretation: confirmation can produce false grounding. (LIKELY, canonical tradition, snippet-only here)**
PP-attachment ("deploy with the new config" — accompaniment vs instrument) plus the finding that misinterpretations *linger past disambiguation*. The gate's paraphrase-back step inherits the vulnerability when both sides share the misparse. Owed: paraphrase-back in *altered syntactic form* (with-phrase confirmed via relative clause) so agreement cannot ride on shared misparsing.

## 4. Design Principles for T-007 (encode these)

**P1. Enumerate relations, not just readings. (F1 + F3 + F7)**
Every flagged ambiguity ships with three slots — term readings (existing), quantifier/negation scope (new, concrete pin: same-X or each-own-X), modifier attachment (new, paraphrase-back in altered form). Empty slots are affirmatively marked N/A, never silently skipped — the N/A mark is what makes the check auditable.

**P2. No antecedent in text = flag, not pass. (F4 + F6)**
Extend the one-antecedent rule to zero-antecedent definites: any "the X" never introduced, any recurrence/factive trigger, any proposition-binding demonstrative gets the same ledger row as an overloaded pronoun. Operate at recall-100%/precision-~60% honesty: over-flag, human discards. TAPHSIR is the template for the pronoun half; the bridging half is still manual.

**P3. Vagueness gets thresholds, force gets a force check. (F5 + F6)**
No discrete readings → switch instruments: threshold probe for gradable adjectives; presupposition-flag column + directive-force check for sentence-level smuggling. Both ride existing machinery (declaration rule, flagged-terms table) — no new ledger owed. Novice-vs-expert discrepancy simulation (F6 method) is the gate test.

## 5. Not-to-Encode (explicit non-goals)

- **N1. Do not re-encode d31128a or 10-03 F1–F7 ground.** SAT cost, high-context probing, C3MOD moderation, grounding, stakes test, escalation stay as built; this file adds triggers, instruments, and the standalone evidence record only.
- **N2. Do not demand full disambiguation.** The nocuous/unnocuous line plus the stakes test: unnocuous ambiguity is correctly ignored. A gate firing on all vagueness is the over-constraint failure the prompt standard warns about.
- **N3. Do not force precision on strategically vague input.** Deliberate vagueness routes to the T-009 sanitizer, never to harder enumeration — enumeration coerces premature commitments that later read as requirements.
- **N4. Do not quote snippet-only numbers as findings.** CLARINET +17%/+39%, RE anaphora 60%/100%/98%, pragmatic-ambiguity 0.75 — single-study priors (LIKELY max), usable for sizing bets, never as design-rationale citations. Rationale cites F1–F7 only.
- **N5. Do not treat confirmation as comprehension.** Shared misparsing survives mutual read-back (F7). Grounding requires paraphrase in altered form, not repetition.
