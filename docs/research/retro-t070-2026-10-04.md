## 3. Top Findings (with confidence + effect sizes)

**F1. Burden predicts attrition — reconfirmed by re-retrieval: objective (length, frequency) plus subjective (effort, salience, sensitivity) proxy factors predict future response rates. (LIKELY, single panel study, direction-only)**

The CPS household-panel study (https://doi.org/10.2478/jos-2022-0049) re-surfaced on a fresh burden query with the same abstract: exploratory factor analysis confirmed five distinct burden factors; regression-tree models showed both objective and subjective factors predicted future response rates, interacting with employment and marital status. Extractor probe failed identically (sciendo E1 empty, jina-attempted) — snippet-only status unchanged. No reversal, no refinement; the original's F1 holds verbatim.

**F2. Fatigue stratifies by role and by calendar: MS 43% vs residents 27% vs faculty 20% (p<0.0001); weekends, holidays, pleasant weather, and salience shocks suppress daily response. (LIKELY, single census + single time-series, numeric rates snippet-verified)**

Surgical-education clerkship-environment surveys across three institutions (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/37913726, n=283+190+374): response 43%/27%/20% by seniority, with lower rates among male respondents, respondents of color, midlevel residents, assistant professors. Dutch Health Surveys 2016–2017 discrete-time survival analysis (http://arxiv.org/abs/2011.02034v2): weekends, holidays, pleasant weather, disease outbreaks, terrorism salience all associate with fewer responses; contextual-only variables alone give satisfactory daily and cumulative rate prediction with no personal data. T-070 corollary (new datum the original lacked): gate-dropout instrumentation must log calendar/context covariates (day-of-week, session length, prior-session gap) before attributing a dropout to gate design — part of abandonment variance is exogenous.

**F3. Turn-level friction always taxes and always buys: all six friction probes raised workload, task duration, AND perceived ownership; recall/recognition gains were selective; the same constraint helped or hindered depending on user goals. (LIKELY, N=24 within-subject lab, extractor-verified primary)**

Within-subject study with six friction probes across three mechanisms — eliciting user contribution, restricting access to generated content, reshaping system output (http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22427v1 — full abstract extracted via 127.0.0.1:8081, ~2,390 chars): every probe increased workload, duration, and ownership versus a conventional chatbot; recall/recognition effects were probe-selective; participants adapted differently and goal-workflow fit determined whether a constraint supported or obstructed involvement. T-070 corollary (the strongest new datum of this retro): protocol gates ARE turn-level friction, so P1's because-clause rule now has a priced trade-off — each gate buys ownership at a workload cost that is certain, while the learning gain is conditional. Encode the budget explicitly: friction without a named ownership/accuracy payoff is pure burden (F3 mechanism for the original's F2 pointlessness driver).

**F4. Disclosure order matters more than disclosure amount: Stranger framing beats Familiar for emotional-first contact; Familiar wins only after low-risk factual rapport. (LIKELY, single mixed-factorial experiment, snippet-only)**

Ephemerality-framing chatbot study across two sessions and two days (http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20464v1): Stranger framing (fresh entity each conversation) raised comfort for emotional disclosure when emotional disclosure came first; Familiar framing (remembers past interactions) felt intrusive unless rapport was first built via low-risk factual disclosure, after which Familiar produced more enjoyment. T-070 corollary (direct support for the original's P2 ordering): the INBOX-exit thin-slice must be factual/low-risk ("what would make this run worth it" about the concrete task), never emotional/identity-level ("what is your ambition"); the full AMBITION dialogue earns Familiar standing only after EXTRACTION has banked factual rapport. Asking identity-level ambition cold is the Stranger-intrusive condition.

**F5. Constructed-preference canon stands unchallenged; two CS transfers sharpen elicitation design: usage-based implicit questions for novices, partial-configuration feedback to cut cognitive load. (CONFIRMED canon; LIKELY transfers, snippet-only)**

Fresh constructed-preference queries returned no canon challenge — Slovic construction, Nisbett & Wilson confabulation, and hypothetical-bias 2–3× all stand as the original left them. New convergent transfers: conversational-recommender study (http://arxiv.org/abs/2111.13463v2) — users without domain knowledge answer usage-based implicit questions ("what will you use it for") where attribute questions fail, with human evaluation confirming generation quality; constructive-elicitation decomposition (http://arxiv.org/abs/1711.08247v2) — inference and feedback over partial configurations drastically reduces user cognitive load versus full-configuration coactive feedback. T-070 corollary: both transfers license the thin-slice + revealed-material design — ask about planned use (implicit), accept partial answers (decomposition), never demand full attribute-style ambition specifications cold.

**F6. Goal-setting primary unrecovered — no new Locke & Latham evidence, no adjudication change. (UNVERIFIED, no-delta)**

The goal-setting query returned robotics/vision goal-conditioning papers plus a goals-as-programs generativity study (http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13242v3, human-judged indistinguishability of model-generated playful goals) — none bearing on difficult-specific-goals performance effects. The original's F6 (aspiration-arousal CONFIRMED-theory, thin-probe license) is therefore carried forward unchanged: no confirmation, no contest, search noise only. Do not cite any new goal-setting number from this sweep.

**F7. SDT instrumentation warning: behaviour-change technologies invoke SDT to optimize engagement with the technology, not internalization of the target behaviour — 50 design suggestions across 15 papers, misdirection pattern. (LIKELY, systematic review, snippet-only)**

SDT-in-BCT review (http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00121v1): 15 ACM papers, 50 SDT-grounded design suggestions extracted, but SDT is leveraged to optimize engagement with the intervention itself rather than the target behaviour — motivation then depends on enjoying the protocol, which wanes. Companion SDT help-seeking design paper (http://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02299v1) applies Basic Psychological Needs Theory to online help-seeking tech. T-070 corollary (refines the original's P1 metric): gate-completion rate is the engagement metric that misdirects; instrument downstream work quality (plan-ambition delta, answer-revision at AMBITION) as the internalization metric, never gate throughput alone.

**F8. Off-the-shelf LLM coding of open-ended motivation answers distorts distributions: only fine-tuning reaches satisfactory performance; prompting effects are model-conditional; category-unequal accuracy reshapes results. (LIKELY, single multilingual study, snippet-only)**

German open-ended survey-participation-motivation coding across LLMs and prompting approaches versus human expert codings (http://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14634v3): performance differs greatly between LLMs with only fine-tuning satisfactory; prompting-approach differences are conditional on the LLM; unequal per-category accuracy produces different categorical distributions without fine-tuning. T-070 corollary (new guard): per-gate abandonment logging (original S2/G4) must not be auto-coded by an off-the-shelf LLM for dropout reasons — human-code the first N abandonment turns (or fine-tune) before trusting any "why users leave" taxonomy; otherwise the measurement instrument manufactures the finding.

## 4. Design Principles for T-070 (encode these)

**P1. Price every question in perceived-burden currency — UPGRADED with a friction budget (original P1 CONFIRMED + F3).**
F3 prices the trade: workload/duration cost is certain, ownership/accuracy gain is conditional on goal-fit. Each gate question keeps its because-clause (original), and now also names its ownership payoff ("this question buys you X control over the plan"). Friction with no named payoff is cut, not kept.

**P2. Early gates cheap and behavioral; late gates expensive and articulatory — CONFIRMED with ordering mechanism (original P2 + F4 + F5).**
F4 supplies the mechanism the original inferred from disclosure theory: factual-before-emotional, use-before-identity. The INBOX-exit thin-slice stays a one-sentence planned-use probe (F5 usage-based form), never an ambition interrogation; full AMBITION stays post-SERIOUSNESS on revealed material. No modification to the order verdict.

**P3. Competence before ambition for low-ambition users — CONFIRMED (original P3 + F7).**
F7 adds the instrumentation constraint: competence is built by genuine progress on the target work, not by gate-completion streaks. Never label users low-ambition (trait language still forbidden). No modification.

**P4. [NEW] Instrument context before attributing dropout — calendar, session, and goal-fit covariates ride along with every abandonment event.**
F2's exogenous variance (weekends/holidays/weather/salience, role seniority gaps) plus F3's goal-dependence mean raw gate-dropout rates confound design signal with context noise. Minimum logged covariates: day/time, session length-so-far, prior-session gap, gate index, question count-so-far. No reorder A/B is interpretable without them.
