The research question for this retro: does a fresh science+general sweep confirm those anchors, refine any claim, or surface deltas g7 never saw — especially 2025 ATD-lifecycle connectivity and cognitive-bias evidence that postdate g7's source set, and whether the regression formalization upgrades from snippet to verified?

## 3. Top Findings (with confidence + effect sizes)

**F1. Postponed architecture work accrues interest — continuous beats catch-up. (LIKELY, extractor-verified today — reconfirmed)**

Lenarduzzi et al. abstract re-extracted verbatim: SME migrating monolith→microservices under Continuous Architecture; initial simplified patterns (direct monolith links, no message bus) forced later deep re-architecting (API gateway / lightweight bus), each round raising new-fault risk; "no studies exist on the impact of postponed activities on the TD, especially in the context of CA and microservices" at publication time (http://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10855v1 — extractor VERIFIED, 3035 chars). g7's F1 mechanism (deferred structural decisions return larger and fault-prone; gate converts lump-sum re-architecture into per-sprint installments) holds unchanged. Single-SME WIP — cap at LIKELY, do not quote time-to-interest numbers (none exist).

**F2. Experts interleave top-down inference with bottom-up execution traces; novices stay bottom-up — the gate must enforce the scarce direction. (LIKELY, extractor-verified today — reconfirmed)**

Burkhardt et al. abstract re-extracted verbatim: "strong evidence of top-down, inference-driven behaviors, as well as multiple guidance in expert comprehension" plus "less use of top-down processes in novice comprehension"; experts consult more files, novices exploit inheritance/composition poorly; static relationships "played a substantial role" for experts (http://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0702002v1 — extractor VERIFIED, 2575 chars). g7's F2 transfer (top-down is the scarce direction; gate spends its time there; order top-down first is load-bearing) survives without modification. Small-N lineage — cap at LIKELY.

**F3. Architecture erosion is checked cheapest at commit time by declared dependency constraints — not by periodic review alone. (LIKELY, extractor-verified today — reconfirmed)**

DepCoL abstract re-extracted verbatim: defines constraints over plugin groups (reducing spec effort), per-plugin constraints, refinements; Eclipse plugin checks consistency "during development… developers are informed about dependency violations immediately" to counteract erosion (http://arxiv.org/abs/1510.08510v1 — extractor VERIFIED, 2602 chars). g7's F3 transfer (P2 constraint ledger + REVIEW as second net; recurring review findings graduate into declared constraints) holds unchanged. Mechanism CONFIRMED as built artifact; erosion-rate numbers still absent — never cite a rate.

**F4. Developers systematically discount future architectural value versus near-term feature work — triage needs an explicit counter-bias. (LIKELY, extractor-verified today — reconfirmed)**

Fagerholm et al. abstract re-extracted verbatim: intertemporal-choice questionnaire, 33 developers / 2 companies, choices between feature vs longer-term architectural investment; "wide-spread temporal discounting with notable differences in individual behavior… consistent with similar studies in consumer behavior… first empirical study on intertemporal choice in SE" (http://arxiv.org/abs/1901.07024v2 — extractor VERIFIED, 2962 chars). g7's F5 transfer (sprint triage is an intertemporal choice under discounting; gate must price the future explicitly — interest estimate + blast-radius if deferred — or discounting decides silently) holds unchanged. Single experiment — cap at LIKELY, do not quote discount rates (none snippet-verified).

**F5. The regression guard is a time-ordered build-chain with a limited test window — the no-regress guarantee is window-bounded by construction. (LIKELY, extractor-verified today — UPGRADED from snippet)**

Formalization re-extracted verbatim: "successive builds as a time-ordered chain, where each build contains the program, requirements, and the accompanying tests… formalize the regression test window between any two builds, which captures the limited time budget… As the time limit is set to infinity and the chain is closed to two builds, the model degenerates to retest-all… validated by directly representing two state-of-the-art agile regression testing algorithms… proof of soundness and completeness" (http://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02810v1 — extractor VERIFIED, 2426 chars). This upgrades g7's F6 from snippet-only to verified-abstract: the window-boundedness is now a formal property, not a practitioner warning. g7's triple-guard (window-bounded suite + smallest-deviation deltas + threshold-triggered refactor) gains a first-principles leg. Still LIKELY (formal model, not field effect size); never write "proven safe" — window + thresholds define the warranty.

**F6. Business-driven prioritization aligns stakeholders and bends the debt curve on high-value assets — the triage ledger needs the business-value column. (LIKELY, snippet-level — reconfirmed neighbor)**

Five-month industrial case: evolving business-driven TD prioritization with active business+technical participation; observed alignment of business criteria and "downward trend in the amount of technical debt that affects high-value business assets"; identified eight business factors affecting prioritisation decisions (http://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09711v2). g7 cited this as the nearest neighbor for F7; this sweep re-returns it independently, confirming the anchor. Business-value column in the triage ledger (P4) thus has the strongest cross-study support of any ledger column — but remains single industrial case, so LIKELY, never a numeric ROI.

**F7. NEW — ATD repayment centralizes dependencies: FAN-IN +57.5% / FAN-OUT +26.7% post-repayment, and ATD files modify less frequently. (UNVERIFIED, snippet-level — new vs g7)**

ATD lifecycle study filtering SATD→ATD via connectivity (FAN-IN/FAN-OUT) and modification frequency across lifecycle stages, Mann-Whitney + Cliff's Delta: FAN-IN up 57.5% mean, FAN-OUT up 26.7% mean after repayment, "suggesting a shift toward centralization and increased architectural complexity after repayment"; ATD files modified less frequently than non-ATD, changes accumulated in high-dependency code (http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15387v2). This is the sharpest new datum of the retro: bottom-up revitalization (repayment) has a structural price — centralization — that g7's installment policy (P3 smallest deviation) now has a mechanism to price against. Cap at UNVERIFIED (snippet-only, single dataset); adopt as "repayment centralizes — check FAN-IN/FAN-OUT deltas," never as a quoted threshold.

## 4. Design Principles for T-058 (encode these)

**P1. One sprint pass, two directions, top-down first — RECONFIRMED.**

F2 (extractor-verified expert interleaving) plus F1 (postponement interest) jointly fix order and cadence: every sprint runs top-down (view → changed parts: which invariants touched, which dependents, which constraints fired) then bottom-up (signals → structure: new smells, new coupling, new constraint candidates). Bottom-up happens without enforcement; top-down does not. Empty findings recorded as explicit "none observed," never skipped silently. No modification from g7.

**P2. Constraints declared, checked at change time — review catches the remainder — RECONFIRMED.**

F3 (DepCoL extractor-verified) makes the mechanism load-bearing: maintain a small declared-constraint set (dependency direction, layering, interface ownership) checked at change time; REVIEW diffusion-debt is the second net for what declarations miss. Recurring review findings graduate into declared constraints. No constraint lives only in prose. No modification from g7.

**P3. Refactor by smallest-deviation installments behind a window-bounded regress guard — STRENGTHENED (F5 upgraded).**

F5's formal build-chain+window result (extractor-verified) makes the warranty explicit: no-regress = (i) window-bounded regression suite per change, (ii) smallest structural delta that pays the debt, (iii) threshold-triggered refactor (quality-gate breach fires work). "No regress" never means proof; it means bounded window + small deltas + gate thresholds. F7 adds the pricing rule: each installment's FAN-IN/FAN-OUT delta is checked because repayment centralizes even when correct. Lump-sum re-architecture is the failure mode F1 prices.

**P4. Triage every old feature on a dated ledger with priced retention — RECONFIRMED and business-anchored.**

F4 (discounting extractor-verified) plus F6 (business-driven prioritization reconfirmed) jointly harden the ledger columns: feature · user value still carried (F6 business column, strongest cross-study leg) · maintenance price + constraint-on-new-work (F4 counter-bias: future priced explicitly) · verdict (keep / degrade-honestly / deprecate-with-date / remove) · next review sprint. Retention without a price is forbidden. Deprecation always carries a date; dateless deprecation is retention. F7's centralization finding adds a structural-price row to the ledger.

## 5. Not-to-Encode (explicit non-goals)

- **N1. Do not encode a canonical sprint-evolution method as adopted practice.** Carried from g7 N1: mapping-study context finds no dominant approach and under-reported costs/failures — the gate is greenfield design (P1–P4), not field adoption. Cite structure, never authority.
- **N2. Do not promise proof of no-regress.** Strengthened by F5: the guarantee is window-bounded (suite + window + thresholds) plus formal degeneration to retest-all only at infinite window. Any gate wording claiming refactor-safety absolutely is the eval-illusion failure; state the window, not the warranty.
- **N3. Do not quote snippet-only numbers as findings.** FAN-IN +57.5% / +26.7%, CI-survey 31-developer proportions, RefBot effectiveness, business-prioritization deltas, bias→ATD counts — single-study priors (LIKELY max), usable for sizing bets, never as design-rationale citations. Rationale cites F1–F6 qualitative structure only.
- **N4. Do not re-encode L1 descent, REVIEW diffusion-debt, or scale-analysis F3 ground.** Bounded descent, propagation checking, and the big→small licence stand; this sweep adds cadence binding (P1), change-time constraints (P2), installment policy with formal window (P3), and priced+business-anchored triage (P4) — deltas only.
- **N5. Do not let the triage ledger become a second backlog.** Carried from g7 N5: every row resolves (verdict) and recurs (date) or it is backlog by another name. Rows without both are rejected at gate time.
- **N6. Do not treat top-down and bottom-up as interchangeable or order-free.** Carried from g7 N6: F2 fixes the order (top-down first) because scarcity differs by direction. A gate running bottom-up-only is the novice profile with a checklist.
- **N7. [NEW] Do not treat dependency metrics as sufficient for ATD triage.** From F7: FAN-IN/FAN-OUT "should be combined with other measures to capture other effects of ATD on maintainability." A triage rule that triggers solely on metric deltas is incomplete by the source's own conclusion.
