## 3. Top Findings (with confidence + effect sizes)

**F1. Retrieval practice effects replicate across formats: testing benefits survive blocked-vs-mixed manipulation, and expanding retrieval trades short-term for long-term gain. (CONFIRMED, directional)**
Blocked-vs-mixed comparison finds mnemonic benefits of retrieval not affected by practice format (https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-016-0641-8); expanding retrieval promotes short-term retention while equally spaced retrieval enhances long-term retention (https://doi.apa.org/doi/10.1037/0278-7393.33.4.704). g2's F1 (retrieval beats restudy) survives; this retro adds the format-invariance boundary. Pooled g still NOT snippet-verified — left UNVERIFIED as in g2/G2.

**F2. The optimal-gap curve replicates in the laboratory with large gains: up to +150% recall at the optimal gap, nonmonotonic throughout. (CONFIRMED, laboratory pair)**
Two three-session studies with test delays to 6 months: gap increases first sharply raise then gradually reduce accuracy; optimal gap improves recall by up to 150% (https://doi.org/10.1027/1618-3169.56.4.236). Converges with Cepeda N>1,350 gap×delay interaction (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02209.x). g2's F3 holds without modification.

**F3. Interleaving shows the desirable-difficulty signature in a real classroom: worse now (+7.8% time, +12% difficulty, −8% assignment), better later (+13% delayed assessment). (LIKELY, n=25 action research)**
High-school chemistry mole-mass conversion, interleaved vs blocked assignment then unannounced interleaved assessment 3 days later (https://nwcommons.nwciowa.edu/education_masters/285). Small-N, single-topic, teacher-as-researcher — discount accordingly; but the sign pattern matches g2's F2 calculus finding exactly. New datum g2 lacked: a concrete classroom interleaving number with the fluency cost attached.

**F4. The misconception problem is on the teacher side too: novice teachers name highlighting/summarising, almost never interleaving, worked examples, or multimodal representations. (LIKELY, N=180 survey)**
Flemish teacher-training survey across open questions, scenarios, and strategy lists (https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2022.996039). g2's P5 (teach subtraction) gains a delivery-side justification: the protocol cannot assume the human arrives knowing the strategy taxonomy (https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-017-0087-y) — it must name low-utility strategies as anti-patterns because even trained novices default to them.

**F5. Feedback on text replicates at g+=0.35 (N=6,124); AI-generated vs human feedback shows no significant performance gap (g=0.25, ns, N=4,813). (LIKELY both sizes)**
Text-feedback meta: elaborate and knowledge-of-correct-response beat knowledge-of-response when given after reading; computer-delivered beats non-computer (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2019.100296). AI-vs-human feedback meta: pooled g=0.25 CI [−0.11; 0.60], perception g=−0.20 ns, I²=95% heterogeneity (https://doi.org/10.1080/01443410.2025.2553639). Protocol corollary: g2's P3 (elaborated, immediate, machine-delivered feedback) is strengthened — machine delivery is not a degraded channel — but the heterogeneity flag (I²=95%) means never quote g=0.25 as "AI equals human."

**F6. Deliberate-practice contest unchanged: falsifiability critique stands, individualization rebuttal stands, no adjudication. (CONTESTED)**
25-year review documents shifting definitions and unfalsifiable edges (https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01134); chess N=178 finds 3× stronger prediction at high individualization/quality (https://doi.org/10.1007/s12144-021-02326-x). Re-sweep returns the same two anchors g2 found — no new meta arrived. g2's refinement ("deliberate practice is not already covered") holds; no further change.

**F7. Metacognitive feedback cuts answer-offloading nearly in half and lifts unaided test performance: OR=0.47 offloading, OR=1.51 test, N=704 preregistered. (LIKELY, extractor-verified primary)**
Fraction-arithmetic practice with on-request LLM assistant then unaided test; effort-reward arm shows no effect (http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20143v1 — full abstract extracted via 127.0.0.1:8081, 2,375 chars). This is the strongest new datum of the retro: a causal, preregistered intervention for exactly T-015's worry, and its mechanism (make offloading implications explicit) is implementable as tripwire-plus-explanation. Convergent illusion result: users predict AI faster with equal completion times and lower felt effort — completion time alone mismeasures efficiency (http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23177v1, N=1,237).

**Supporting (LIKELY/UNVERIFIED):**
- S1. Agentic-AI review converges on intentional friction + dynamic scaffolding + human-in-the-loop as the design triad (http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04543) — LIKELY; independent convergence with g2's P1/P4, new citation.
- S2. DeBiasMe triad (deliberate friction, bidirectional intervention, adaptive scaffolding) (http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16770v1) — UNVERIFIED; position paper, adopt as design vocabulary only.

## 4. Design Principles for T-015 (encode these)

**P1. Attempt before answer — unchanged roof rule (g2 P1 CONFIRMED).**
F1's format-invariance plus F7's offloading cut jointly strengthen the sequencing rule: retrieval first, assistance after. No modification.

**P2. Space reps across cycles; never trust within-cycle fluency — unchanged (g2 P2 CONFIRMED).**
F2 (+150% optimal gap) and F3 (+13% delayed at −8% assignment cost) both repeat the worse-now-better-later signature. Encode the signature explicitly wherever reps are scheduled: "if the rep feels fluent, it is massing."

**P3. Feedback after every rep: elaborated, immediate, machine-delivered — STRENGTHENED.**
F5 adds the AI-parity meta: machine delivery is not second-class. Add the heterogeneity guard: never promise parity, promise elaboration. F7 adds the content requirement: feedback must include the offloading implication ("here is what you skipped by asking first"), not just correctness.

**P4. Calibrate difficulty to expertise — unchanged (g2 P4 CONFIRMED).**
No new expertise-reversal datum in this sweep; F6's individualization finding continues to govern. No modification.

**P5. Teach subtraction — UPGRADED from exhortation to necessity.**
F4 (teachers themselves misjudge strategies) makes P5 load-bearing rather than decorative: the protocol must name rereading/highlighting/massed recap as anti-patterns at the point of use, because the human's prior defaults there.

**P6. [NEW] Make offloading explicit at the tripwire — metacognitive feedback, not restriction.**
F7's causal result (OR=0.47/1.51): when the abdication tripwire fires, state the implication ("answering this for you removes your retrieval rep for X; attempt first, then I elaborate") rather than refusing or silently complying. Incentive framing alone (effort reward) showed no effect — do not encode points/streaks as the lever.

## 5. Not-to-Encode (explicit non-goals)

- **N1. Do not build testing theater.** Unchanged from g2 N1 — F1's format-invariance does not license quizzes at every gate.
- **N2. Do not encode the 10,000-hour myth or any fixed practice quantity.** Unchanged from g2 N2 — F6 still contested, no dose function.
- **N3. Do not quote AI-feedback parity (g=0.25) as equivalence.** NEW guard from F5: CI crosses zero with I²=95%; the encodable claim is "elaborated machine feedback works," never "AI equals human."
- **N4. Do not diagnose helplessness or any trait from passivity.** Unchanged from g2 N4 — F7 operationalizes on offloading behavior counts, never labels.
- **N5. Do not re-litigate guidance scaffolding.** Unchanged from g2 N5 — scaffolding/fading mechanics stay in guidance-dynamic-2026-09-30.md.
- **N6. Do not encode effort-rewards as the anti-deskilling lever.** NEW guard from F7: the reward arm showed no effect; metacognitive feedback is the mechanism.
