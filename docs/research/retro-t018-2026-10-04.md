## 1. Problem Statement

T-018 asks for full general research on human learning — the durable evidence base the protocol *assumes* whenever it claims to "grow the user," "teach," or "make learning cheap." The disposition history is BUILT-without-sweep: F-017 shipped learning deltas as inline citations (Roediger & Karpicke 2006 testing effect, Chi 1989 self-explanation, generation effect, then c981891's metacognitive decoupling arXiv:2603.29681, expertise-reversal calibration, Kapur productive failure, guidance meta d=0.50–0.71), and g2 saved the standalone synthesis for T-015+T-018 jointly (retrieval CONFIRMED, spacing d=0.54, gap scaling, feedback g+=0.35, deliberate practice CONTESTED, RIF sequencing roof, P1–P5/N1–N5). Retro-t015 then re-verified g2 and added metacognitive feedback (OR=0.47/1.51, N=704) plus AI-feedback parity (g=0.25 ns, I²=95%).

The remainder — this sweep's question — is everything general-learning science holds *outside* that retrieval/spacing/feedback/DP/RIF core: (a) the theory families the protocol implicitly leans on but never names (cognitivism, constructivism, humanism/SDT, social learning); (b) adult-learning specifics (andragogy, self-direction — protocol users are working adults, not schoolchildren); (c) motivation mechanics (SDT need satisfaction with numbers); (d) cognitive-load architecture (working-memory bottleneck, worked examples, expertise reversal, collaborative load); (e) transfer (does anything learned here travel anywhere — test-enhanced d=0.40 vs far-transfer null); (f) metacognition/SRL as a tutoring target (planning/monitoring/debugging moves, compulsive-intervention bias); (g) the tutoring-effectiveness ceiling (Bloom 2-sigma and its revision). Each maps to one design principle in §4.

## 3. Top Findings (with confidence + effect sizes)

**F1. Successful cognitive learning rides on affective capacity: hope, agency, self-efficacy, self-regulation, and goal-setting — not cognition alone. (LIKELY, thesis convergence)**
The Warwick mixed-method programme (https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/193822/1/WRAP_Theses_Skelton_2024.pdf, snippet) finds success factors aligning with Dewey (habitual reflection, educator–pupil interaction) plus Snyder hope, Bandura agency/self-efficacy, Dweck/Zimmerman self-reflection/regulation, and Ryan–Deci goal-setting — proposing a Positive Learning Psychology in which cognitive learning depends on "lightening" affective load via strengthened positive resources (Fredrickson broaden-and-build). Transfer: the protocol's stance/probe machinery (SERIOUSNESS feeling probes, engaging default) is the affective-load instrument, not decoration — a run that raises threat without raising agency taxes exactly the load this thesis says blocks learning.

**F2. Adult learners are not large schoolchildren: six andragogical assumptions govern whether they learn at all — and AI delegation rewrites what "self-directed" means. (LIKELY, canonical + extractor-verified preprint)**
Knowles/Holton/Swanson six (need-to-know, self-concept, experience, readiness, orientation, motivation; http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.11803/594, snippet; https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ace.3, snippet) plus the self-direction literature (https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v1n3p66; https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sbspro.2015.01.832; https://doi.org/10.1007/s12186-019-09228-x, all snippets) converge on experience-as-resource and problem-orientation. The extractor-verified 2026 revisit (https://osf.io/dh4mx_v1) reframes under AI delegation: behavioural independence ≠ self-direction; five dimensions (need/goal ownership, delegation control, epistemic calibration, cognitive recoverability + transfer, motivational autonomy) plus three applicability gates decide whether delegated cognition stays self-directed. Transfer: the protocol's AMBITION/EXTRACTION gates (goal ownership, experience elicitation, readiness probing) already enact 4 of 6 assumptions; the missing two are the preprint's — delegation control and recoverability ("if I let the agent do this, can I still do it / check it after?").

**F3. Need-supportive environments move motivation at g≈0.41 overall — autonomy support largest (g=0.84), relatedness smallest (g=0.28) — but only as a bundle, and technology engagement ≠ behaviour change. (LIKELY, meta snippet + extractor-verified review)**
The SDT health-intervention meta (k=74, 80% RCTs; https://doi.org/10.1080/17437199.2018.1534071, snippet): perceived autonomy support g=0.84, autonomy g=0.81, competence g=0.63, relatedness g=0.28, motivation g=0.41; one-to-one > group for competence (0.96 vs 0.28), adults > children (0.95 vs 0.11); meta-regression finds individual strategies have limited independent impact — the bundle matters. The extractor-verified BCT review (http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00121v1, 15 ACM papers, 50 design suggestions) adds the failure mode: SDT is routinely used to optimise engagement *with the technology* rather than the target behaviour, so motivation evaporates with novelty. Transfer, with the adult>child moderator noted: per-run SDT bundle (autonomy = declared delegation + choice; competence = elaborated feedback + fading; relatedness = continuity seed, not flattery) aimed at the *work*, never at protocol-love.

**F4. Working memory is the bottleneck; load has three kinds; worked examples cut extraneous load early — and the same support that helps novices harms experts. (LIKELY, canonical framework)**
AMEE Guide 86 (https://doi.org/10.3109/0142159x.2014.889290, snippet): sensory/WM/LTM architecture, WM processes limited elements at once; intrinsic (essential), extraneous (non-essential), germane (strategy) loads; techniques — decrease extraneous (worked examples), titrate intrinsic (simplify without decontextualising), dedicate spare WM to germane; expertise-reversal as the progression rule. Collaborative CLT (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9277-y, snippet) adds collective working memory with transactive costs; the expertise-reversal canon (https://link.springer.com/10.1007/978-1-4419-8126-4_12, snippet) is the boundary. Transfer: g2 P4 (calibrate difficulty to expertise) is the F4 corollary — this sweep supplies the mechanism language (name the load kind being cut in each gate: readings-gate cuts extraneous; INBOX simplification titrates intrinsic; explain-back spends germane).

**F5. Testing transfers at d=0.40 — but far transfer of trained skills is approximately zero once bias is controlled. Both claims hold simultaneously. (LIKELY both, snippet-only)**
For transfer: Pan & Rickard first comprehensive meta of transfer-of-test-enhanced-learning (192 effects, 122 experiments, N=10,382, 40 years; https://osf.io/4qpyn_v1, snippet, extraction E3-empty): d=0.40 [0.31, 0.50] vs re-exposure control; largest across test formats, application/inference questions, medical diagnoses, mediator/related cues; weakest to rearranged S-R items, untested study materials, worked examples; response congruency + elaborated retrieval + initial-test performance moderate; PET-PEESE/selection models shrink intercepts toward zero without moderators. Against far transfer: chess/music/WM-training meta (https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721417712760, snippet — effects inverse to design quality) and the second-order meta (k up to 233; https://online.ucpress.edu/collabra/article/5/1/18/113004/Near-and-Far-Transfer-in-Cognitive-Training-A, snippet, extraction E1-empty): near transfer yes (population-moderated), far ≈ 0 with placebo/bias controlled — "lack of generalisation is an invariant of human cognition." Transfer of the transfer literature: the protocol should promise *near* transfer (same-project reps, standing decisions, retrieval-shaped ratification) and never sell general cognitive enhancement; worked-example transfer is the documented-weakest case (F5-for), so examples teach format, reps teach skill.

**F6. Metacognitive tutoring is move selection over planning/monitoring/debugging/evaluating — and current LLMs are compulsively interventionist at exactly the wrong moments. (LIKELY, new primaries)**
MetaCLASS (http://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02457v1, snippet): 11 interpretable coach moves, two-phase plan-then-dialogue, 1,015 conversations / 7,711 turns with turn-level labels; best model 43.2% next-move accuracy; compulsive-intervention bias — 41.7% of turns need silence, models predict silence 4.2% of the time while over-predicting high-intervention moves. Irec (http://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20156v1, snippet) formalises the alternative paradigm: context-triggered retrieval of the learner's own past insights as JIT scaffolding (JITAI framework, knowledge-graph + hybrid retrieval + Socratic module). Transfer: the tripwire/Light machinery must learn *silence* as a move — the MetaCLASS number makes g2 N1 (no testing theater) load-bearing and measurable (silence-precision as a harness metric); Irec is the shape of the continuation seed done right (recall *my* insight at the moment of need, not a generic recap).

**F7. The tutoring ceiling is lower — and more instructional — than the legend: tutoring ≈ well-built software approaches, and active engagement can out-carry retrieval in the same design. (LIKELY–UNVERIFIED, practitioner summaries + single study)**
The Bloom-revision systematic review (https://nintil.com/bloom-sigma/, general-category snippet): tutoring matches software-based approaches rather than doubling them; mastery/tutoring/direct-instruction ordering is narrower than myth. The dental 2×2 (N=120, n=30/cell; https://doi.org/10.18297/etd/3582, snippet): ENG and SRP both significant, ENG larger; ENG+SRP interaction — SRP adds d=0.48 with ENG but d=1.39 without; ENG adds d=0.63 with SRP but d=2.10 without (sub-additive, compensatory pattern). Transfer, stated as humility: the protocol is a software approach with tutoring moves — its learning claim should be pitched at " tutored-software," never "two-sigma tutor"; and engagement (attempt, generation, self-explanation) is the first lever, retrieval spacing the second — matching g2's attempt-before-answer roof from an independent direction.

**Supporting (LIKELY/UNVERIFIED):**
- S1. Explanations help human-AI joint retrieval — but only when actually helpful; incorrect/unhelpful explanations degrade below no-explanation (http://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03285v4, snippet) — LIKELY; elaboration-quality guard for P3.
- S2. Workplace self-directed learning is experienced as obliged + creativity-enhanced + fast/flexible + tied-to-work (https://doi.org/10.1007/s12186-019-09228-x, snippet) — LIKELY; the protocol's work-embedded reps fit how ICT workers already frame learning.

## 4. Design Principles for T-018 (encode these)

**P1. Name the theory family at each learning move. [F1/F4 roof]**
Every gate that teaches (EXTRACTION declaration, STRATEGY ratification, tripwire question, REFLECT recap) states which family it serves in one clause: schema-building (cognitivist/CLT — cut extraneous, titrate intrinsic), construction (constructivist — attempt before structure), or will (humanist/SDT — autonomy/competence). A learning move with no family is decoration. This is F1's "cognition rides affect" plus F4's load language made auditable.

**P2. Enact all six andragogy assumptions; add the two delegation questions. [F2 transfer]**
Need-to-know (why this gate exists, one line), self-concept (human decides scope — declared delegation), experience (elicit before informing), readiness (probe-gated depth), orientation (problem-first, never subject-first), motivation (internalise the goal, not the tool — F3's warning). Plus the preprint's pair at every agent-does-it moment: *who controls this delegation, and can you recover/check it after?* If either answer is no, the move is doing, not teaching.

**P3. Ship motivation as a bundle aimed at the work — autonomy + competence + relatedness, never points. [F3 transfer]**
Autonomy: declared delegation + bounded choice + the right to redirect. Competence: elaborated immediate feedback (g2 P3) + fading with demonstration + near-transfer reps (F5). Relatedness: continuation across cycles (durable record, recognised recap — Irec-shaped, F6), never praise/grades (g2 N3 stands). Retro-t015 N6 stands with reinforcement: effort-rewards showed no effect — do not encode streaks/points as the motivational lever; F3's meta-regression says single techniques don't move outcomes alone.

**P4. Label the load kind in gate text. [F4 transfer]**
When a gate simplifies, say which load it cuts: readings-gate and declaration rules cut *extraneous*; INBOX simplification and survival-set titrate *intrinsic*; explain-back and self-explanation spend *germane*. Novices get worked examples; experts get blanks (expertise reversal — g2 P4 unchanged). Collaborative moments (multi-agent review, human+agent co-design) budget transactive costs explicitly — joint attention is load too.

**P5. Promise near transfer only; design reps for congruency. [F5 transfer]**
The encodable transfer claim: retrieval-shaped reps transfer across formats and to application/inference within the project (d=0.40 direction, snippet-only — never quoted as a number in gate text per N2). Design rules from the moderators: keep response congruency high (rep in the format of future use), elaborate the retrieval (not bare answer), ensure initial-test success before spacing. Never promise far transfer (general reasoning, intelligence, cross-domain skill) — the second-order null forbids it. Worked examples are the weakest transfer vehicle: use them to teach format, reps to teach skill.

**P6. Tutor silence as a scored move; recall my insight, not a recap. [F6 transfer]**
Add *no-intervention* to the coach move set and score it: the tripwire fires questions, but the default between reps is silence while the human attempts (MetaCLASS 41.7%-need-silence as the calibration prior, never quoted as spec). Continuation seeds follow Irec: surface the human's own past insight at the moment of need (verbatim one-liner + where it was learned), not a summarised report. Compulsive elaboration is the failure mode with a number; restraint is the mechanism.

**P7. Pitch the learning claim at tutored-software, not two-sigma tutor. [F7 humility]**
Protocol copy (QUICKSTART, EXPLAINER, gate headers) claims engagement + retrieval + feedback effects, never Bloom-scale transformation. The ordering rule: engagement first (attempt/generate/explain), retrieval-spacing second — the dental interaction pattern says engagement carries further alone and the pair is sub-additive, so a run short on time protects the attempt, not the schedule.

## 5. Not-to-Encode (explicit non-goals)

Do not re-encode g2 or retro-t015.** Retrieval/spacing/gap-scaling/strategy-taxonomy/text-feedback sizes, deliberate-practice contest, RIF roof, metacognitive-feedback tripwire wording (OR=0.47/1.51), AI-parity heterogeneity guard, and interleaving numbers live in `g2-learning-standalone-2026-10-04.md` / `retro-t015-2026-10-04.md`. This file owns theories, andragogy, SDT bundle, load labelling, transfer scoping, tutor-silence, and ceiling-humility only.
- **N2. Do not quote snippet-only numbers as findings.** Pan–Rickard d=0.40, SDT meta g=0.84/0.81/0.63/0.28/0.41, dental d=0.48–2.10, chess g=0.338/0.382/0.248, MetaCLASS 43.2%/41.7%/4.2% are single-sweep priors (LIKELY max), usable for sizing bets, never as design-rationale citations. Rationale cites F-directions only.
- **N3. Do not promise far transfer or general cognitive enhancement.** The second-order null (F5-against) forbids marketing the protocol as making users smarter in general; the encodable promise is durable project skill + better learning habits, both near-transfer.
- **N4. Do not diagnose motivation, helplessness, or any trait.** SDT needs are design inputs (support autonomy/competence/relatedness), never user labels; g2 N4 / retro-t015 N4 extend here — the tripwire fires on behaviour counts, never on "unmotivated" or "dependent."
- **N5. Do not gamify the protocol.** Points, streaks, badges, and praise-as-feedback contradict F3 (bundle-only effects, engagement-with-tool failure mode) and retro-t015 N6 (reward arm null). Motivation ships as ownership + competence evidence + continuity, not as game layer.
- **N6. Do not re-litigate guidance scaffolding or testing-theater bans.** Scaffolding/fading mechanics stay in `guidance-dynamic-2026-09-30.md`; the no-quiz-every-gate rule (g2 N1) and no-10,000-hour rule (g2 N2) stand unmodified.
