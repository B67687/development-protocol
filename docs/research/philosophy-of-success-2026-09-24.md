## 1. Classical traditions

### 1.1 Aristotelian eudaimonia and virtue

**Eudaimonia = flourishing activity, not a state — CONFIRMED.** SEP Aristotle Ethics (§2) + Wikipedia Nicomachean Ethics lede agree: the highest good is desirable for itself, all else for its sake; the function (*ergon*) argument (NE I.7, 1097b22–1098a20) makes the human good the activity of the rational soul in accordance with virtue over a full life.
→ Success is a pattern of activity sustained over time, not a threshold crossed once; the protocol should evaluate trajectories, not single outputs.

**Virtue by habituation + practical wisdom — CONFIRMED.** Ethical virtues are acquired by habit ("we become just by doing just acts," NE II.1) and steered by *phronesis* (practical wisdom); the doctrine of the mean (NE II.6) locates each virtue between excess and deficiency (SEP §§4–6 + Wikipedia NE Books II–III, VI).
→ Deliberation templates cannot substitute for trained judgment; habituation (repeated practice with feedback) is the mechanism.

**External goods needed as instruments, mistakable for ends — CONFIRMED.** Friends, wealth, honor, health are instruments of virtuous activity (NE 1099a31–b6); severe misfortune can diminish happiness (SEP §§2–3.2).
→ Resource failures (tools, context, time) genuinely degrade outcomes; the protocol must distinguish instrument-failure from agent-failure.

**Why efforts fail (Aristotle):** mistaking instruments (wealth, honor, pleasure) for the end; lacking upbringing/habituation (NE I, 1095b4–6 — CONFIRMED via SEP §3.1); lacking *phronesis* to read each occasion. Synthesis sentence: UNVERIFIED (synthesis) on CONFIRMED premises.

### 1.2 Stoicism: the control dichotomy, premeditatio malorum

**Dichotomy of control — CONFIRMED.** *Enchiridion* 1: "Some things are up to us and some are not up to us" — *prohairesis* (judgments, impulses, assent) is ours; body, property, reputation are not (Wikipedia Enchiridion verbatim + SEP Epictetus §§4.3–4.4). Only virtue is good, vice bad, all else "indifferents" to be used well (SEP Stoicism §§4.1–4.3).
→ The single most protocol-relevant classical doctrine: sort every concern into controllable / not, act unconditionally on the first, accept the second.

**Emotions as false judgments about externals — CONFIRMED** (SEP Epictetus §4.5 + SEP Stoicism §4.7). *Enchiridion* 5: "What upsets people is not things themselves but their judgments about the things" (Wikipedia verbatim).
→ Post-failure affect is data about a judgment, not about the world; re-examine the assent, not just the plan.

**Pursue ends "with reservation" — LIKELY.** *Hupexhairesis*: exert fully while knowing the outcome has no ultimate value; Epictetus's ball-game analogy (*Discourses* 2.5; SEP Epictetus §§4.4, 4.7 quotes 2.5.1, 2.5.6).
→ Commit to the attempt, reserve judgment on the outcome — the ancestor of "commit to process, detach from result."

**Premeditatio malorum as a fixed ancient practice — UNVERIFIED (likely modern label).** Rehearsal of evils (poverty, exile, death, loss) appears across Seneca/Marcus/Epictetus, but no fetched SEP passage uses that exact Latin phrase; the slogan form is late/modern.
→ Cite the practice (negative visualization), not the slogan, as ancient.

**Key surviving texts — CONFIRMED:** Epictetus *Discourses* (via Arrian, 4 of 8 books) + *Enchiridion* (53-chapter abridgment); Seneca *Letters*; Marcus Aurelius *Meditations* (SEP Stoicism §1.3 + SEP Epictetus §1 + Wikipedia).
Sources: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/stoicism/, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epictetus/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enchiridion_of_Epictetus, https://classics.mit.edu/Epictetus/epicench.html

### 1.3 Taoism: wu wei, non-striving, resistance

**Wu wei = effortless action aligned with natural order, not passivity — CONFIRMED** (SEP Laozi lede: "naturalness (*ziran*) and nonaction (*wuwei*)" + Wikipedia Wu wei lede).
→ Forcing against the propensity of a situation multiplies cost; reduce mediation, align with it.

**Daodejing: 81 chapters, composite ~4th–3rd c. BCE — CONFIRMED** (SEP Laozi §§2–3 + Wikipedia Tao Te Ching infobox).
→ Chapter-number citations below are standard numbering, translations vary: cap at LIKELY.

**Soft overcomes hard; forcing rebounds — LIKELY.** Water imagery (Ch.8: highest good like water, contends with none); Ch.48 *wei wuwei* ("do less until nothing is left undone"); Ch.76 soft/supple = life, hard/rigid = death (Wikipedia Wu wei + Tao Te Ching themes; SEP notes freedom from tyranny of desire).
→ Resistance is information: where effort meets disproportionate resistance, the approach — not the goal — is usually wrong.

**Why efforts fail (Taoism):** forcing, naming, craving, over-legislating reverse *fan* (reversal) dynamics — what is forced rebounds. UNVERIFIED (synthesis) from LIKELY premises.
Sources: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/laozi/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu_wei, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tao_Te_Ching

### 1.4 Zen and Buddhism: right effort, non-attachment

**Four Noble Truths + non-self — CONFIRMED** (SEP Buddha §2; Wikipedia Noble Eightfold Path lede). Suffering originates in craving/ignorance/appropriation as "I/mine"; no permanent self among the five *skandhas*.
→ The self that "succeeds" is conventionally useful, ultimately a causal series — take outcomes seriously without identifying with them.

**Right Effort (*samma-vayama*) = guard conditions, not force outcomes — LIKELY.** The four exertions: prevent unarisen unwholesome, abandon arisen unwholesome, arouse unarisen wholesome, maintain arisen wholesome (Wikipedia §2.9 heading fetched, body not re-verified; Tricycle URL unfetched).
→ Effort belongs on conditions (setup, habits, inputs), exactly where results are not guaranteed.

**Zen: practice as everyday expression — LIKELY (single-source).** Sitting (*zazen*), *satori*, Rinzai koan vs. Soto *shikantaza*, buddha-nature in the everyday lifeworld (SEP Japanese Zen excerpt via search; popular citations like "Mu," Dogen's *Genjokoan*, "chop wood, carry water" UNVERIFIED — do not use without checking).
→ Enlightenment metaphors aside, the operational claim is ordinary: do the present task completely.

**Non-attachment ≠ indifference — LIKELY.** Craving, not sensation, is the target; allow pleasant/painful without appropriation (SEP Buddha origination analysis).
Sources: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/buddha/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noble_Eightfold_Path

### 1.5 Confucian self-cultivation

**Goal = *junzi* via lifelong cultivation practices — CONFIRMED** (SEP Confucius §§3–4: study, ritual *li*, music, filial piety *xiao*, humaneness *ren*, righteousness *yi*, trustworthiness *xin*; *Analects* as core source, SEP §2).
→ Success is role-excellence built from small daily trainings, not insight events.

**Ritual psychology: *li* reforms desires and trains emotions — CONFIRMED** (SEP §3: Analects 10.3, 10.8, 15.1, 3.14, 3.17 sheep example).
→ Form precedes feeling: imposed forms (checklists, gates, reviews) can shape judgment from the outside in — the strongest classical warrant for protocol machinery.

**Govern by virtue, not punishment — LIKELY** (SEP §5; Analects 2.3, 12.11 not body-verified). Verse numbers (12.1, 2.3, 1.2, 2.15) are LIKELY (standard numbering, pages unfetched).
→ Coercive rules produce evasion; exemplary standards produce emulation. A protocol people route around has failed Confucian-style.
Sources: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/confucius/, http://www.acmuller.net/con-dao/analects.html (returned, unfetched)

### 1.6 Epicureanism

**Goal = *aponia* + *ataraxia* via true pleasure — CONFIRMED** (SEP Epicurus lede + Wikipedia Epicureanism lede).
→ Cap downside (remove pain/disturbance) before chasing upside; durable calm beats intense peaks.

**False physics + unlimited desires as the failure mechanism — CONFIRMED premises.** Unacknowledged fear of death/punishment drives irrational extremes; atomism + soul mortality removes afterlife fear (SEP lede). Desire taxonomy (natural-necessary / natural-unnecessary / vain) is LIKELY (SEP headings imply; bodies truncated).
→ Audit desires: most suffering comes from absolutized wants (wealth, status, luxury), not from unmet needs.

**Gods unconcerned; withdraw from politics; friendship essential — CONFIRMED** (SEP lede + Wikipedia). Sources: letters to Herodotus/Menoeceus, Principal Doctrines, Vatican Sayings, Lucretius (SEP §1 — CONFIRMED list); *Tetrapharmakos* wording LIKELY (not body-verified).
Sources: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epicurus/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicureanism

## 2. Modern philosophy

### 2.1 Pragmatism: James and Dewey

**Meaning = conceivable practical effects — CONFIRMED.** Peirce's Pragmatic Maxim (1878, EP1:132 quoted verbatim in SEP Pragmatism); James (1907 *Pragmatism*) reformulates it as dispute-dissolution: no practical difference, no real dispute (SEP Pragmatism §§2–3).
→ If two approaches differ in no observable consequence, stop debating them; test what differs.

**Truth as cash-value / warranted assertibility — CONFIRMED doctrine, LIKELY wording details.** James Lecture VI: ideas become true insofar as they lead us into useful relation with reality ("cash-value" phrasing widely quoted, full text unfetched — do not cite page numbers). Dewey's instrumentalism: ideas are instruments converting indeterminate → determinate situations; truth = what is settled enough to resource further inquiry, always revisable (SEP Dewey §§4.1, 4.3, fetched verbatim; *Logic: The Theory of Inquiry* 1938 five-phase inquiry pattern — CONFIRMED).
→ Beliefs are tools with expiry dates; re-test load-bearing assumptions when the situation changes.

**Why efforts fail (pragmatism):** reifying abstractions, treating doubt as theoretical instead of inquiring experimentally, judging ideas by correspondence to fixed Reality rather than revisable consequences. Synthesis: UNVERIFIED on CONFIRMED premises.
Sources: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/pragmatism/, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dewey/

### 2.2 Existentialism: Kierkegaard, Sartre, Camus

**Kierkegaard: despair = failure to synthesize; faith = commitment through the absurd — CONFIRMED.** Self as synthesis of finite/infinite, temporal/eternal, necessity/possibility (*Sickness Unto Death* 1849; SEP Kierkegaard §2). Abraham as knight of faith, "teleological suspension of the ethical" (*Fear and Trembling* 1843; SEP Kierkegaard + SEP Existentialism §5.2). "Leap of faith" as a formula is LIKELY paraphrase, not direct quote.
→ Two failure poles: despair of possibility (endless imagining, no commitment) and despair of necessity (fatalism, no imagination). The protocol-relevant one is the first: analysis that never becomes decision.

**Sartre: bad faith = denying facticity or transcendence — CONFIRMED.** *Being and Nothingness* 1943 Ch.2: waiter and gambler examples; "existence precedes essence"; later revised to freedom-in-situation (SEP Existentialism §4 + SEP Sartre §4.2).
→ "I had no choice" and "that's just who I am" are both bad faith; even not-choosing is a choice. Own the decision, including delegation decisions.

**Camus: the absurd + revolt without consolation — CONFIRMED.** Absurd = appetite for meaning meeting the world's "unreasonable silence"; reject physical and philosophical suicide; answer = revolt, freedom, passion; "One must imagine Sisyphus happy" / "the struggle itself toward the heights is enough to fill a man's heart" (SEP Camus §§1, 3.1–3.4 + Wikipedia Myth of Sisyphus, both quoting).
→ No final vindication arrives; persist lucidly without demanding the universe confirm the plan.

**Why efforts fail (existentialism):** evasion of choice (conformity to the crowd, habit-mechanical life, hope/afterlife appeals) instead of lucid commitment. Synthesis: UNVERIFIED on CONFIRMED premises.
Sources: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/existentialism/, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sartre/, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kierkegaard/, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/camus/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Myth_of_Sisyphus

### 2.3 Nietzsche: amor fati, self-overcoming, eternal recurrence

**Amor fati — CONFIRMED.** "Want nothing to be different, not forward, not backward, not in all eternity" (*Ecce Homo* II:10); "learn to see as beautiful what is necessary" (*Gay Science* 276) (SEP Nietzsche §3.2.2 + Wikipedia Amor fati, both quoting verbatim).
→ Treat failures as material to affirm and use, not stains to explain away.

**Eternal recurrence as affirmation-test — CONFIRMED function, LIKELY-contested cosmology.** *Gay Science* 341 demon thought-experiment; *Zarathustra* elaboration; SEP notes the cosmology-vs-therapeutic-myth scholarly split explicitly (SEP Nietzsche §6.3 + fall2026 life-works entry).
→ Decision heuristic, testable use: "would I will this sprint/approach again exactly?" If not, change it now.

**Will to power mostly notebook doctrine; self-overcoming is the published theme — CONFIRMED.** Elisabeth's *Will to Power* compilation is editorially misleading (SEP Nietzsche §1); *Selbstüberwindung* ("man is something to be overcome") is the Zarathustra theme; higher type affirms life including suffering (SEP nietzsche-moral-political, fetched verbatim).
→ Aim each cycle at surpassing the previous standard, not at matching a fixed bar.

**Why efforts fail (Nietzsche):** ressentiment, herd morality, No-saying — escape into otherworlds, pity, or fixed truths instead of willing this life as-is. Synthesis: UNVERIFIED on CONFIRMED premises.
Sources: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nietzsche/, https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2026/entries/nietzsche-moral-political/, https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2026/entries/nietzsche-life-works/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amor_fati

### 2.4 Ikigai: the Venn meme versus the Japanese original

**Venn diagram (love / good-at / world-needs / paid-for) is a 2014 Western adaptation, not Japanese — CONFIRMED** (Wikipedia Ikigai §Diagram + Marc Winn's own 2025 self-account at theviewinside.me: one-word swap on Zuzunaga's 2011 purpose diagram after Buettner's 2009 Okinawa TED; Forbes/Economist coverage corroborates).
→ Never encode the four-circle Venn as "ikigai"; it is a career-advice meme wearing a Japanese word.

**Japanese original: felt daily worth, multiple and mundane — CONFIRMED.** *Ikigai* = felt sense life is worth living; Kamiya *Ikigai ni tsuite* (1966, untranslated, no diagram) distinguished sources of meaning vs. *ikigai-kan* (felt sense); Japanese researchers (Hasegawa: *seikatsu* everyday life not *jinsei* lifetime; Kumano 4-factor/5-mechanism model, no consensual definition; Mathews: *ittaikan* commitment + *jiko jitsugen* self-realization) regard the diagram as misleading — Hasegawa/Kumano details LIKELY (single-snippet sources).
→ Durable motivation attaches to small recurring daily meanings and roles, not to one grand Purpose.

**Health link (lower mortality) — LIKELY, effect sizes UNVERIFIED.** Sone et al. 2008 Ohsaki + Tanno et al. 2009 JACC via Wikipedia refs; primaries unfetched.
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikigai

### 2.5 Jung on control and flow, and the Taoist debt

**Jung's debt to Richard Wilhelm is extensive and explicit — CONFIRMED.** Wilhelm sent Jung the *Secret of the Golden Flower* (German 1929) and the *I Ching* translation; Jung wrote the 1929 commentary + 19-page foreword to Wilhelm/Baynes *I Ching* (1949/1951); friendship from 1923 Zurich meeting; *Memories, Dreams, Reflections* pp.373–377 (Wikipedia Secret of the Golden Flower + Synchronicity, both fetched; foreword PDF at https://livingtao.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/I-CHING-Carl-Gustav-Jung.pdf).
→ The East–West bridge on non-forcing has a documented scholarly channel, not just vibes.

**Synchronicity via the I Ching: reading the moment vs. forcing causality — CONFIRMED.** Hexagram as "exponent of the moment"; synchronicity "takes coincidence as meaning something more than mere chance"; causality "merely statistical truth" (Jung foreword PDF verbatim + Wikipedia Synchronicity on the 1952 Jung–Pauli statement).
→ When causal levers are exhausted, attend to the configuration of the moment rather than pushing harder on one lever.

**No Jung doctrine of "control vs. flow" exists — UNVERIFIED as Jung doctrine (correct the record).** No SEP/Wikipedia source attributes a control/flow theory to Jung. Verifiable nearby claims: relativizing ego-control (active imagination, "art of letting things happen," Taoist *wu-wei* as parallel to ego→Self decentering). Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi's "flow" (1975) must NOT be attributed to Jung — LIKELY confusion of Jung + *wu-wei* + modern flow in the requesting note.
→ Use Jung–Wilhelm as precedent for "stop forcing, attend," never as a flow-psychology citation.
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synchronicity, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_of_the_Golden_Flower

## 3. Empirical correlates A: flow, grit, deliberate practice, goal-setting

### 3.1 Flow (Csikszentmihalyi)

**Origin (1975) + antecedent triad — CONFIRMED.** *Beyond Boredom and Anxiety* (1975), artist interviews, water-current metaphor (fetched Wikipedia Flow). Preconditions: (a) challenge–skill balance, (b) clear proximal goals, (c) immediate feedback — later reformulation treats these as antecedents, the other six as the experience (Wikipedia Components + two Springer snippets). Full 9-dimension model (Jackson & Csikszentmihalyi 1999) CONFIRMED.
→ Design work for the triad (matched difficulty, clear next goal, fast feedback); the state itself cannot be commanded.

**Literature is correlational/descriptive; no causal effect size comparable to goal-setting d's — CONFIRMED.** ESM + FSS/FSS-2 scales; applications across education/sport/music/work/gaming (Wikipedia Applications/Effects + 2026 flow/coping network paper).

**"Crisis point" measurement critique — CONFIRMED.** Swann et al. 2018: 9-dimension model not mechanistic, conflates antecedents/experience/outcomes, discriminant-validity problems (Springer PFS snippet quoting p.249 + PMC Polish PFS validation echo). Peifer & Engeser 2021 "no gold standard" is LIKELY (single snippet).
→ Invoke flow's antecedents as design targets; never claim flow itself as a success lever without the Swann/Norsworthy caveats.
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flow_(psychology), https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41042-023-00092-8, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12680258/

### 3.2 Grit (Duckworth) and its critiques

**Definition: perseverance + passion for long-term goals; PE + CI facets — CONFIRMED** (Duckworth et al. 2007 JPSP 92:1087–1101; Grit-O → Grit-S; fetched Wikipedia Grit + Credé abstract + Gwern full-text mirror).

**Original evidence: r=.25 GPA, West Point retention, Spelling Bee — CONFIRMED** (fetched Wikipedia Scientific findings, citing Duckworth 2007). Passion-defense (Jachimowicz et al. 2018 PNAS, 127 studies n=45,485) LIKELY.

**Credé meta-analysis: the critique with numbers — CONFIRMED.** Credé, Tynan & Harms 2017 JPSP 113(3):492–511 (https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000102): 584 effects, 88 samples, N=66,807 — higher-order grit structure NOT confirmed; grit–conscientiousness ρ≈.84 (k=22, N=18,826); PE ≫ CI in criterion validity; "jangle fallacy" (twin-study genetic r=.86; Schmidt et al. 2018 CFA: PE indistinguishable from conscientiousness factor; Credé 2018 *Educational Researcher* — all CONFIRMED via PubMed/APA/Gwern/SAGE/Wikipedia).
→ Never encode "grit" as a construct; perseverance-of-effort behaviors are the usable residue, and they largely re-describe conscientiousness.
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grit_(personality_trait), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27845531/, https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/personality/conscientiousness/2016-crede.pdf, https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/0013189X18801322, https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.1803561115

### 3.3 Deliberate practice (Ericsson) and the range critiques

**Deliberate practice requires a teacher-designed, feedback-rich, solitary-repetition loop — CONFIRMED.** Ericsson, Krampe & Tesch-Römer 1993: master teacher → diagnosis → solitary practice; five criteria incl. (5) designed + supervised by a teacher, without which it is merely "purposeful practice" (fetched Wikipedia Practice + fetched Ericsson & Harwell 2019 https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02396/full).
→ Most "practice" in knowledge work fails criterion (5); build the teacher function (reviewer, rubric, worked examples) before scheduling the reps.

**10,000 hours is a popularization, not Ericsson's claim — CONFIRMED.** Berlin violinists' *average* by age 20, amplified by Gladwell *Outliers* (2008); Gladwell himself conceded the split ("hard practice guy vs. soft practice guy," 2016 interview).

**The variance numbers, both sides — CONFIRMED.** Macnamara, Hambrick & Oswald 2014 (88 studies): practice explains 14% overall; 26% games, 21% music, 18% sports, 4% education, <1% professions (https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614535810). Hambrick et al. 2014: 34% chess, 30% music. Ericsson & Harwell 2019 reanalysis (k=14, strict criteria): r=.54 → 29% uncorrected, 61% attenuation-corrected at .60 reliability — with the caveat that 61% hinges on low reliability and deliberate-vs-purposeful differed *ns* (p=.64). Counter: high-individualization practice effect >3× average (2021 chess study, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12144-021-02326-x).
→ Practice quality (individualization, feedback) dominates practice quantity; report both numbers with the definitional dispute, never the slogan.

**Epstein *Range* specifics (late specialization etc.) — UNVERIFIED here** (book unfetched; compatible peer-reviewed numbers above are the CONFIRMED substitutes).
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practice_(learning_method), https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01134/full

### 3.4 Goal-setting theory (Locke & Latham) — the strongest quantitative leg

**Specific difficult goals beat vague/easy ones — CONFIRMED.** Locke 1968 → 1990 program; difficulty–performance positive-linear to ability/commitment limits (fetched Wikipedia Goal_setting + 2002 retrospective PDF + 2006 Current Directions).

**Effect sizes — CONFIRMED.** Goal difficulty d=.52–.82; specific-difficult vs. do-your-best d=.42–.80; generality: 100+ tasks, 40,000+ participants, ≥8 countries (Locke & Latham 2002 retrospective, Stanford-hosted PDF). 1981 vote-count ~90% favoring specific-challenging; calibration ≈ 90th percentile when motivation binds (fetched Wikipedia). Adjacent Epton et al. 2017 behavior-change meta-analysis d=.34 is LIKELY (single DOI record).

**Complexity moderator + learning-goal remedy — CONFIRMED.** Wood, Mento & Locke 1987: difficulty d=.48 most-complex vs .67 least-complex; specific-vs-best d=.41 complex vs .77 simple. Performance goals can *harm* novel complex learning (Kanfer & Ackerman); remedy = specific difficult *learning* goals (Winters & Latham 1996) + proximal sub-goals (Latham & Seijts 1999).

**Moderators: commitment, feedback, complexity, resources — CONFIRMED.** Mediators: direction, effort, persistence, strategy (2019 50-year chapter + fetched Wikipedia). Goal source (assigned/participative/self-set) generally *ns* at equal difficulty.

**Failure modes — CONFIRMED with reply.** Goal failure depresses affect/motivation (https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.704790); high goals → moral disengagement when commitment high (https://doi.org/10.1002/job.2467); tunnel vision; inconsistent sport application (https://doi.org/10.1080/1750984x.2021.1901298). Locke & Latham's reply: mismatched measures, no feedback/commitment, wrong goal type, no proximal goals, restricted range.
→ For novel/complex work set *learning* goals + proximal sub-goals, never bare outcome targets.
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_setting, https://med.stanford.edu/content/dam/sm/s-spire/documents/PD.locke-and-latham-retrospective_Paper.pdf, https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/current-directions/j.1467-8721.2006.00449.x/, https://www.decisionskills.com/uploads/5/1/6/0/5160560/locke_latham_2019_the_development_of_goal_setting_theory_50_years.pdf

### 4.1 Locus of control (Rotter)

**Internal vs. external generalized expectancy; a continuum — CONFIRMED** (fetched Wikipedia Locus_of_control; Rotter 1954/1966; 23+6 forced-choice I-E scale).

## 5. Convergence — what the traditions and the evidence agree on

1. **The target is action in the world, not the state of the mind. — CONFIRMED as a convergence.** Stoicism directs effort at *prohairesis*; ACT at overt, values-consistent behaviour; Confucian *li* at repeated practice; Dewey at inquiry-as-doing; goal-setting at specific goals with feedback. The quantitative legs are goal-setting (difficulty d=.52–.82) and ACT (A-Tjak 2015 overall g=0.57).
2. **Effort fails when it is aimed at what the person does not control — outcomes, other people, or their own feelings. — CONFIRMED** for the Stoic dichotomy (Enchiridion 1) and for ACT's experiential avoidance (control *of private events* is the problem); **LIKELY** for the Taoist *fan* (forcing) and Zen right effort (guard the conditions, do not force the fruit).
3. **Specific goals with feedback beat vague aspiration and "do your best". — CONFIRMED**, the strongest quantitative claim in the file (specific-difficult vs do-your-best d=.42–.80; 100+ tasks, 40,000+ participants).
4. **On novel and complex work, learning goals beat performance goals. — CONFIRMED** (Wood, Mento & Locke 1987; performance targets can *harm* novel complex learning; the remedy is a specific difficult learning goal plus proximal sub-goals).
5. **The design of the environment and of practice dominates willpower. — CONFIRMED.** Deliberate practice needs a teacher-designed, feedback-rich loop (Ericsson, Krampe & Tesch-Römer 1993); practice explains only 14% of variance overall (Macnamara 2014) but individualised practice runs above 3× the average (2021 chess); locus of control works through visible action–result contingency (ρ=.22–.32, Judge & Bono 2001), not through telling anyone to feel more internal.
6. **The character words that carry the most cultural weight are usually re-descriptions of something more basic. — CONFIRMED** for grit (ρ≈.84 with conscientiousness; Credé et al. 2017) and **LIKELY** for "10,000 hours" (a popularisation Gladwell amplified, not Ericsson's claim).

## 6. Genuine conflicts, and how each side argues

1. **Strive versus do not strive.** Goal-setting says specific difficult goals raise performance; wu wei and right effort say forcing produces the *fan* reversal. The traditions are aimed at *how* the effort is held (attachment to outcome) rather than at whether effort is made — wu wei is CONFIRMED as non-passive, effortless action aligned with the grain. Both are satisfiable at once, and the protocol already leans this way (specific goals for the commitment, open exploration inside the gate).
2. **Control versus acceptance.** ACT says control of private events is the problem and control of behaviour is the answer; Stoicism splits control by object. These agree with each other and conflict only with the popular reading of "just let it flow", which the protocol must not adopt as an instruction.
3. **Outcome targets versus process.** The complexity moderator says outcome targets can harm novel complex work, while institutions grade outcomes. This one is genuinely unresolved and must be held as two goals rather than one: an outcome goal for the commitment, a learning goal for the search.
4. **What counts as evidence.** The traditions are unfalsifiable in their metaphysical parts; the empirical side is correlational in flow and contested in grit and practice. A protocol must not import a tradition's claim at a meta-analysis's confidence.

## 7. Testable versus poetry

**May be encoded as principle, because the evidence bears it:** specific goals with feedback; learning goals plus proximal sub-goals on novel work; making the action–result contingency visible; controlling behaviour rather than feelings; recognition over recall; overrun multipliers for time estimates; practice quality (feedback, individualisation) over practice quantity.

**Poetry — framing for a human, never a rule for the agent:** amor fati; the eternal recurrence as cosmology; satori; the absurd; eudaimonia as a metaphysical telos; Jungian individuation as a control-or-flow doctrine; "the flow the universe means for you". These may be quoted to a person; they must not become a gate, a check, or an instruction.

## 8. Candidate principles (and the not-to-encode list)

Three that survive, each already half-present in the protocol:

1. **Aim effort at what the run controls, and name the rest as residue.** Stoic dichotomy plus ACT's overt control, and the protocol's own residue rule. The gate card's *Your part* line already does this; one line at SERIOUSNESS should name what the human will *not* be asked to control.
2. **A learning goal for the search, an outcome goal for the commitment.** Goal-setting's complexity moderator. AMBITION already asks for a falsifiable hypothesis; it should say which kind of goal it is setting.
3. **Make the action–result contingency visible at every gate.** Locus of control (ρ=.22–.32) plus deliberate practice's feedback criterion. This is the empirical justification for the gate cards and the visible-closure design shipped in T-040.

**Not to encode:** grit as a separate lever; 10,000 hours as a target; the ikigai four-circle Venn; any Jung control-or-flow doctrine; points, badges, streaks or leaderboards; "let go and flow" as an instruction to the agent; and any single-source claim from this file's UNVERIFIED set.
