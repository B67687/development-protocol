# COMMUNICATION-BURDEN Research: Question Fatigue, Gate Ordering & the Low-Ambition User (Retro T-070)

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Retro:** T-070 — protocol relies too much on communication, too forceful; most people don't know what they want / low ambition; users don't like questioning; ambition gate perhaps earlier
- **Downstream question:** should AMBITION move earlier (INBOX→AMBITION→EXTRACTION?) vs current INBOX→EXTRACTION→SERIOUSNESS→AMBITION
- **Core pattern:** Every question the protocol asks is priced in the user's perceived-burden currency, not in the protocol's information-value currency. Burden is driven by perceived pointlessness and disorganization more than by question count; preferences (including ambition) are constructed at elicitation time, not retrieved — so early heavyweight ambition interrogation taxes the users least able to pay it. Order gates cheap→expensive, behavioral→articulatory, and scaffold ambition late rather than demanding it early.
- **Method:** SearXNG self-hosted (127.0.0.1:8888, categories=science primary, then general), Tavily fallback where SearXNG returned empty (chatbot-UX, progressive-disclosure, SDT-correlate queries), Trafilatura extractor sidecar (127.0.0.1:8081 POST /extract) probes on 3 content targets (1 full-verified, 1 partial-verified, 1 failed), confidence labels per claim.
- **Confidence scheme:** CONFIRMED = replicated/meta-analytic or canonical primary source; LIKELY = single-study or strong convergent theory; UNVERIFIED = plausible/practitioner, needs verification.

## 1. Problem Statement

T-070 records four converging complaints: (a) the protocol communicates too much — too many questions, too forceful a tone; (b) most users arrive without knowing what they want and with low ambition; (c) users dislike being questioned; (d) the AMBITION gate may belong earlier in the sequence. These interact: if users cannot articulate wants (b) and resent interrogation (c), then each additional question (a) raises dropout risk while yielding confabulated answers — and moving AMBITION earlier (d) could either fix orientation early or detonate the funnel at its widest point. The research question: what does the literature say about question/communication burden and fatigue, constructed preferences, ambition as a construct, the low-ambition (amotivated) user experience, and staged-disclosure ordering — and which gate order does that evidence license?

## 3. Top Findings (with confidence + effect sizes)

**F1. Burden predicts attrition — and subjective burden (effort, salience, sensitivity) matters alongside objective burden (length, frequency). (LIKELY, single panel study, direction-only ES)**
CPS household-panel study (https://doi.org/10.2478/jos-2022-0049, snippet; extractor probe failed — sciendo blocked, E1): exploratory factor analysis confirmed proxy burden measures form five distinct factors; regression-tree models showed both objective and subjective proxy burden factors predicted future response rates, interacting with respondent characteristics (employment, marital status). T-070 corollary: dropout is not a pure function of question count — a short but effortful/pointless-feeling gate can cost more than a longer salient one. Instrument *perceived* burden per gate, not just gate length.

**F2. Perceived burden detaches from actual length: repetition, disorganization, and pointlessness are the drivers; reframing length changes felt burden at fixed actual length. (LIKELY, single study, snippet-only)**
Beyond-survey-length study (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0282423X261451321, snippet): respondents' own accounts of "burden" centered on repetition, disorganization, and perceived pointlessness; experimentally dissociated frames of reference produced significant differences in both perceived length and perceived burden regardless of actual length. T-070 corollary: this is the mechanism behind "too forceful" — repeated re-asks, disorganized gate jumps, and questions whose purpose is invisible to the user are what register as interrogation. A "because-clause" on each question is a burden intervention, not politeness.

**F3. Over-questioning a population collapses response rates fast: 51.0% → 34.5% under surge conditions; reminders raise rate but not sample diversity. (LIKELY, single census + single experiment, numeric rates verified in snippet)**
Neurosurgery survey census (https://doi.org/10.3389/fsurg.2021.690680, snippet; 255 articles, 32.3% pandemic-era): pandemic-era surveys drew more responses in absolute terms (247.0 vs 206.4, p=0.01) but far lower rates (34.5% vs 51.0%, p<0.001) with wider distribution — the signature of fatigue. Antwerp web-survey experiment (https://doi.org/10.1080/13645579.2016.1185255, snippet; N=15,651): extra reminders raised response rates but did not diversify the sample. Invitation-design experiment (https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/index.php/index/article/view/7777, snippet; young adults 18–35): shorter invitation text + mid-placed link raised participation and lowered non-response bias. T-070 corollary: (i) fatigue effects are large (∼16pp) and fast (months, not years); (ii) nagging (reminders/re-asks) recovers quantity without recovering representativeness — re-prompting a user who disengaged does not recover *that user's* signal; (iii) front-door brevity is the cheapest burden cut available.

**F4. Conversational agents lose users at exactly the protocol's failure points: forced restarts, redundant clarification, keyword-level understanding. (LIKELY, practitioner + 1 highly-cited review; NN/g partial extractor-verified)**
NN/g chatbot UX (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/chatbots, extractor partial-verified 48KB): users forced through whole decision trees for simple queries, forced restarts after one unexpected answer ("No" to body-style preference → bot stopped, start over), and redundant per-turn clarification (Eno re-asking which account after *each* question; keyword "transaction" matched instead of the actual question). Zheng 2022 conversational-HAI review (https://dl.acm.org/doi/fullHtml/10.1145/3491102.3501855, snippet; cited 198×): CAs repeatedly fail to understand or help users achieve intended goals — pragmatic failure, not cosmetic. Practitioner priors (UNVERIFIED, direction-only): one bad chatbot experience loses ∼30% of customers (Forrester via Userlytics); 56% of unhappy customers leave silently (no complaint signal); linear 8+-step tours complete at ∼53% vs ∼75% contextual (Chameleon 15M benchmark via vendor blogs). T-070 corollary: every gate restart and every re-asked question is a F4 event; silent leavers mean gate-dropout instrumentation cannot rely on complaints — log abandonment turns per gate (cf. chatbot "drop-off point mapping").

**F5. Users not knowing what they want is the default condition: preferences are constructed at elicitation, reports are confabulated from causal theories, stated intensity overstates real commitment by 2–3×. (CONFIRMED, canon + companion-verified)**
Slovic construction-of-preference canon (https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/identifier/CBO9780511618031A052/type/book_part, snippet): preferences constructed from labile values, not retrieved. In-repo companion (self-knowledge-spectrum-2026-09-24.md, already verified): Nisbett & Wilson (1977) — no introspective access to higher-order processes, reports rest on a priori causal theories (CONFIRMED); hypothetical-bias meta-evidence — stated WTP exceeds real WTP 2–3× (Loomis 2011, 447 cites; Schmidt & Bijmolt 2019, 77 studies/115 ES, mean +21%; CONFIRMED direction); revealed layer outranks stated by precedent (Samuelson 1938). T-070 corollary (the load-bearing one for gate ordering): a cold AMBITION question asked before any behavioral evidence exists harvests confabulation at maximum burden cost. Ambition answers become trustworthy only after the user has done something real to react to — which is an argument for ambition *late*, scaffolded by revealed material, not earlier.

**F6. Ambition is state-like and elicitable, not just trait: self-set aspiration levels arouse achievement motive more than instructions do; difficult specific goals govern performance. (CONFIRMED theory; LIKELY single-study arousal effect; UNVERIFIED numbers)**
Level-of-aspiration tradition (Hoppe/Lewin lineage; anatomy review https://linkinghub.elsevier.com/retrieve/pii/B9781483230436500150, snippet); aspiration-setting vs achievement-instruction experiments (https://doi.org/10.5334/pb.582, snippet): personal level-of-aspiration setting showed more arousal power on performance than both neutral and standard achievement-instruction conditions, in both sexes — a purer achievement-motive arousal. Goal-setting theory (Locke & Latham; new-directions review https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8721.2006.00449.x, snippet): specific difficult goals, mediators/moderators, generality across people/tasks/countries; contemporary meta-analysis (Minnesota dissertation 2025, https://hdl.handle.net/11299/281731, snippet — numbers UNVERIFIED). T-070 corollary: the early-ambition case has real substance — a lightweight self-set aspiration *probe* can arouse motive and frame processing (cf. just-right F5: goal frame changes processing). But F6 licenses a *thin* early probe, not the full AMBITION gate: elicitation works because it is self-set and low-cost, which is exactly what a heavyweight early gate would destroy.

**F7. Low ambition is amotivation — absence of intent from thwarted competence/autonomy — and competence is the strongest lever: competence > autonomy > relatedness; autonomy–amotivation ρ≈−.34, competence–amotivation ρ≈−.38. (CONFIRMED ranking, meta-analytic; numeric ρ snippet-only → LIKELY)**
Bureau et al. pathways meta-analysis (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8935530, extractor full-verified): 144 studies, N>79,000 — competence is the most positive predictor of self-determined motivation, then autonomy, then relatedness; teacher autonomy support out-predicts parental. Correlate pattern (Tavily snippet of SDT overview page, snippet-only): autonomy–amotivation ρ=−.34 (k=29), competence–amotivation ρ=−.38 (k=37), autonomy–intrinsic ρ=+.57 (k=52), autonomy support–intrinsic r≈.54. Ryan & Deci 2000 continuum (https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf, snippet): amotivation = non-regulation — not valuing the activity, not feeling competent, or not expecting outcomes; sits at the far pole from intrinsic motivation. Self-concordance model (Sheldon & Elliot 1999, via https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/research/goals-values-and-aspirations, snippet): self-concordant goals → sustained striving and well-being. T-070 corollary: interrogating an amotivated user about ambition thwarts autonomy further (pressure) without touching competence — the exact wrong order. Competence-first (a small genuine win inside EXTRACTION) must precede ambition-talk; ambition questions asked of users with zero felt competence return defensive low-aiming answers that then anchor the whole run.

**F8. Ordering evidence favors cheap→expensive, behavioral→articulatory, contextual over linear — with a narrow exception for load-bearing upfront setup. (LIKELY direction; practitioner numbers UNVERIFIED)**
Progressive-disclosure canon (NN/g; vendor syntheses https://usertourkit.com/blog/progressive-disclosure-onboarding, https://www.digia.tech/post/progressive-disclosure-mobile-ux, all Tavily-snippet, UNVERIFIED): disclose to readiness, first success fast; linear tours recalled 2–3 of 10 features shown. Practitioner deltas (priors only, do not quote as findings): contextual 75% vs linear 53% completion, +30% paid conversion (Chameleon); 8→4 fields +120% completion (Baymard); tutorial carousels <30% completion. Staged/conditional/contextual disclosure taxonomy (https://lollypop.design/blog/2025/may/progressive-disclosure, snippet). The exception (same sources): front-loaded setup wins when downstream work is impossible without the input (trust/compliance/data requirements) — the honest test for "AMBITION early" is whether EXTRACTION/SERIOUSNESS are truly impossible without ambition known. They are not: extraction produces the material ambition reacts to, and seriousness (cheap, behavioral) screens without articulation cost. T-070 verdict: evidence favors **keep AMBITION late; add a thin early aspiration probe; make SERIOUSNESS the early gate**.

**Supporting (LIKELY/UNVERIFIED):**
- S1. Short invitation + mid-placed link reduces non-response bias, not just raises rate (Konstanz experiment, snippet — LIKELY): front-door brevity improves *who* stays, which matters more than how many for a protocol whose failure mode is compliant-but-disengaged users.
- S2. Chatbot drop-off mapping as method: abandonment-turn logging is the standard practitioner instrument (CleverX/Velaro guides, UNVERIFIED) — adopt per-gate dropout logging before any reorder A/B.
- S3. Achievement-goals ↔ intrinsic motivation meta-analytic link (Rawsthorne & Elliot 1999, via SDT bibliography page, snippet — LIKELY): approach-oriented ambition framing should outperform avoidance-oriented ("don't waste effort") framing inside the AMBITION gate.

## 4. Design Principles for T-070 (encode these)

**P1. Price every question in perceived-burden currency: pointlessness × disorganization × repetition — never in count alone (F1 + F2 + F4).**
Each gate question must carry a visible because-clause (why this, why now, what it changes downstream); no re-asks of answered material (ledger-checked before asking); no gate restarts (F4: resume mid-flow, never top-of-tree). Burden metric per gate = user-visible purpose + single-ask guarantee, audited in review.

**P2. Early gates cheap and behavioral; late gates expensive and articulatory — SERIOUSNESS early, AMBITION late, with a one-question aspiration thin-slice at INBOX exit (F5 + F6 + F8).**
The thin-slice ("in one sentence: what would make this run worth it?") costs ∼1 question, is self-set (F6 arousal without pressure), and orients extraction — but it is *not* a gate: no fail, no follow-up interrogation, no anchoring downstream judgments on it. The full AMBITION dialogue stays post-SERIOUSNESS where revealed material exists for the user to react to (F5: construction needs material). Seriousness screens on behavior (showing up, doing the small thing), never on articulated ambition.

**P3. Competence before ambition for low-ambition users: manufacture one genuine win inside EXTRACTION before any ambition-talk (F7 + F3).**
Amotivation is thwarted competence/autonomy, not a character verdict — never label users low-ambition (trait language forbidden). Routing: first real extracted item → quick visible progress → then ambition question lands on felt competence and returns higher aiming. Interrogating ambition at zero competence anchors the run low (F7) and spends the fatigue budget (F3) where answers are least trustworthy (F5).

## 5. Not-to-Encode (explicit non-goals)

- **N1. Do not move the full AMBITION gate earlier on goal-framing evidence alone.** F6's arousal effect is thin-probe evidence; F5 (construction needs material) + F3 (fatigue) + F7 (competence-first) jointly dominate for the heavyweight dialogue. Early-move requires winning a reorder A/B (see G4), not a literature vote.
- **N2. Do not trait-label low ambition.** Amotivation is a state on the SDT continuum (F7); "low-ambition users" as a type reifies a momentary need state and licenses forceful handling — the exact T-070 complaint.
- **N3. Do not quote practitioner numbers as findings.** Chameleon 75%/53%, Baymard +120%, Forrester 30%, 56% silent-leaver figures are vendor priors (UNVERIFIED) — usable for sizing bets, never as design rationale citations. Rationale cites F1–F8 only.
- **N4. Do not fix dropout with reminders/re-prompts.** F3: reminders raise rate without diversifying signal — re-asking a disengaged user recovers compliance, not truth. Fix the gate, not the user.
- **N5. Do not ask "in general" ambition questions.** Companion-document constraint (affective forecasting: ask the about-question, never the in-general question): "what is your ambition?" invites impact-bias-grade overstatement or defensive lowballing; ask about the concrete extracted material instead.

## 6. Open Gaps

- **G1.** Numeric effect sizes for burden→attrition unrecovered (JOS 2022 regression-tree ES, Sage framing-study ES: snippet-only; sciendo extractor probe failed E1) — retrieve full texts via institutional access before quoting numbers.
- **G2.** No direct literature on ambition-question timing ("early vs late elicitation order") located across science + general + Tavily — the F8 transfer from progressive disclosure is cross-domain analogy, capped at LIKELY. Primary protocol work required.
- **G3.** Question-behavior / mere-measurement effect (does asking about ambition change ambition?) not searched — candidate next sweep; cuts both ways for the thin-slice proposal.
- **G4.** Reorder A/B unrun and uninstrumented: per-gate abandonment logging (S2) must exist before INBOX→AMBITION→EXTRACTION can be tested against current order. Proposed metrics: gate dropout rate, answer-revision rate at AMBITION (confabulation proxy), downstream plan-ambition delta.
- **G5.** Thin-slice validity unknown: does a one-sentence INBOX-exit aspiration predict anything (task completion, final satisfaction) or merely add one more question? Validate against G4 instrumentation before hardening into protocol.
- **G6.** Agent-transfer gap: all F-evidence is human respondents; LLM-protocol interrogation dynamics (where the "interviewer" has infinite patience and the user knows it) unmeasured. Candidate: gate-count A/B with dropout + satisfaction endpoints.
