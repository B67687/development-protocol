- **Thought:** T-002 — "Whether the protocol is too rigid, and whether when the user interrupts with new thoughts it can balance between discussing with the user and using the protocol."
- **Question:** After A4 parks the interruption and resumes the gate, what governs the *balance* — when to discuss the interruption now vs defer it, how much discussion is owed, and how to resume without residue?
- **Method:** SearXNG self-hosted (`127.0.0.1:8888`, `categories=science` primary, `general` follow-ups), Trafilatura extractor sidecar (`POST 127.0.0.1:8081/extract`) — 8 probes, 5 full-verified, 3 failed hard (see §8).
- **Confidence scheme:** CONFIRMED = replicated/meta-analytic or canonical primary source; LIKELY = single-study or strong convergent theory; UNVERIFIED = plausible/practitioner, needs verification. Snippet-only numbers stay LIKELY max and never enter design rationale (§5 N4).

## 1. Problem Statement

T-002 asks whether the protocol is too rigid when the user interrupts with a new thought mid-run: can it balance *discussing with the user* against *staying in protocol*? The disposition history splits the thought in two. The first half is BUILT: the raw-first rule (T-010) guarantees the interruption is attended to raw before any framework, and A4 (`a3f20b3`, `steps/RULES.md` § Interruption) defines the live handler — park the new thought verbatim in `THOUGHT_LOG.md` with disposition parked, state which gate is paused and what it resumes with, then continue; the interruption never replaces the active gate's exit criteria, and a correction to the active gate's own artifact is explicitly *not* an interruption (handled in place).

The remainder — this sweep's question — is the balance A4 does not adjudicate: (a) *triage* — which interruptions deserve immediate discussion vs verbatim parking (urgent redirect vs tangent vs correction-misclassified); (b) *dose* — how much discussion a parked interruption is owed before resuming (one acknowledging sentence vs a full detour); (c) *resumption fidelity* — how the paused gate restarts without attention residue from the detour. A4 parks correctly; the literature below governs what happens between the park line and the resume line.

## 3. Top Findings (with confidence + effect sizes)

**F1. Interruptions at high-workload moments cost more; task structure predicts the cost. (LIKELY, one abstract extractor-verified today)**
The Frontiers 2024 experiment (six-subtask sequence, interruptions before subtask 2/3/4) finds higher resumption costs before the third subtask than before the second or fourth — a timing effect consistent with chunking into triplets — while the ACM task-structure model shows resumption lag is predictable from structural characteristics with "reasonably high accuracy" across tasks (https://doi.org/10.1145/1124772.1124882, snippet; https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1465323, abstract verified). Transfer: a gate mid-subtask (high workload, unchunked state) is the worst moment to discuss an interruption; acknowledging + parking immediately and discussing at the next subtask/gate boundary is the cost-minimal shape. The protocol's gates *are* subtask boundaries — A4's "continue the run" already points the right way; the remainder is making boundary-deferral explicit.

**F2. Writing a ready-to-resume plan cuts attention residue and its exhaustion path. (LIKELY, single field study, snippet-only)**
The diary intervention study (N = 50 × 4 = 200 occasions; https://doi.org/10.1037/str0000362) finds intrusions raise attention residue and exhaustion, while an employee-centered planning intervention — writing ready-to-resume plans — reduces residue and attenuates the residue→exhaustion link. Transfer: A4's "state which gate is paused and what it will resume with" is exactly a ready-to-resume plan. The falsifiable upgrade: require the resume line to name *progress + next micro-step* ("paused at SERIOUSNESS probe 3/5, resume with probe 4"), not just the gate name — the study's mechanism is progress-articulation, not label-articulation.

**F3. Self-interruptions disrupt more than external ones; practitioners rate switching as disruptive as interruption. (LIKELY, snippets)**
"External interruptions are less disruptive than self-interruptions" (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2016.06.037); 141 surveyed developers perceive planned/unplanned task switching as disruptive as random interruptions, with flow loss and invisible time-fragmentation costs (http://arxiv.org/abs/1805.05504v1, abstract verified). Transfer, with one inversion noted in N3: in protocol runs the *user's* interruption is principal-initiated (more like external-to-agent), while the *agent* volunteering discussion is self-initiation-like — so the agent should default to the cheaper direction (brief park, continue) and let the human pull toward discussion, not push it.

**F4. The defer-vs-interrupt decision is a utility balance, not a rule. (CONFIRMED framework; UNVERIFIED numbers)**
Horvitz's attention-sensitive alerting (http://arxiv.org/abs/1301.6707v1, abstract verified) frames mediation as balancing context-sensitive deferral cost against interruption cost under uncertainty, with the Priorities system inferring message criticality; the deferral-policy and intelligent-notification survey literature (http://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10171, abstract verified) adds that content factors dominate contextual ones in user preference explanations, and users prefer suppressing over deferring alerts. Transfer: the T-002 remainder needs a *criticality test*, not a fixed dose — park-and-continue is the default, immediate discussion requires passing a bar (does this change the active gate's exit criteria or invalidate its premises?).

**F5. Track task initiative separately from dialogue initiative. (LIKELY, convergent snippets)**
The initiative-tracking model (https://doi.org/10.3115/976909.979651) distinguishes who drives the *task* from who drives the *dialogue*, with cue-based prediction gains of 2–4pp (task) and 8–13pp (dialogue); controllable mixed-initiative generation (http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04147v1, abstract verified) shows policy-prescribed intents can steer LLM responses with gains over fine-tuning. Transfer: A4 conflates the two — parking the thought cedes neither, but the resume line should record both explicitly ("task initiative stays with gate G; dialogue initiative returned to user re: T-###"). This makes the balance auditable instead of felt.

**F6. Off-topic utterances are classifiable at ~84% precision — triage assistance is feasible, not fantasy. (LIKELY, single study, bib verified)**
The SIGDIAL topic-shift model (https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2021.sigdial-1.17) fine-tunes XLNet-base to separate major-topic from off-topic utterances at 84% precision, with a major/minor/off-topic extension toward system-initiative diversion rectification. Transfer: the correction-vs-interruption call (A4's counterexample line) is the automatable part of triage — "does this utterance address the active gate's artifact?" is a topic-relevance judgment of exactly this shape. Human keeps the call; the classifier shape is the future harness.

**F7. Interrupted tasks are better remembered — parking works *because* of Zeigarnik, but rumination is the price. (LIKELY, theoretical convergence)**
The Zeigarnik effect (interrupted > completed recall; https://doi.org/10.1891/emdr-d-20-00011) plus prospective-memory interruption studies (https://doi.org/10.1037//0012-1649.37.3.418) jointly predict: a verbatim-parked thought will stay salient without rehearsal (good — nothing is lost), *and* will intrude as residue while the gate continues (bad — F2's mechanism). Transfer: the pair F2+F7 is the whole remainder in miniature — park verbatim to exploit Zeigarnik persistence, attach a ready-to-resume line to contain its rumination cost. Neither half works alone.

**Supporting (LIKELY/UNVERIFIED):**
- S1. Resumption cues feel helpful without moving objective metrics (lab N=15: no effect on time/errors, users still wanted them; field N=16: interactive test cues preferred) — supports writing the resume line for *perceived* continuity even when the gate would resume fine without it. (https://doi.org/10.3390/mti6010002, blocked → UNVERIFIED details, LIKELY direction)
- S2. Self-intermission (user taking a break) beats application-switching and fixed-interval delivery as the least-interruptive moment (thesis work) — transfer: if the user pauses the run themselves, that is the cheapest moment to surface parked thoughts back. (snippet-only → UNVERIFIED)
- S3. Users explain notification preferences via *content* more than *context* (experience-sampling N=35) — transfer: triage should ask "what is this about" before "when did it arrive." (snippet → LIKELY)

## 4. Design Principles for the T-002 Remainder (encode these)

**P1. Triage before parking: correction, redirect, or tangent. [Triage]**
Every mid-gate user turn gets a one-line silent classification before A4 fires: (a) *correction* — addresses the active gate's artifact → handle in place (A4 counterexample, unchanged); (b) *redirect* — passes the criticality bar (changes exit criteria or invalidates premises) → discuss now, gate pauses explicitly; (c) *tangent* — everything else → A4 park-verbatim + one acknowledging sentence + continue. The bar for (b) is F4's utility test, stated aloud when invoked ("pausing SERIOUSNESS because this changes its exit criteria: …").

**P2. Every park ships a ready-to-resume line: progress + next micro-step. [F2 transfer]**
Extend A4's "state which gate is paused and what it will resume with" from label to plan: name completed progress and the exact next micro-step ("paused at SERIOUSNESS probe 3/5 — probes 1–2 felt COMMIT, resume with probe 4 (schedule-vs-drop)"). This is the Leroy intervention in protocol form; Zeigarnik (F7) keeps the parked thought alive, the resume line keeps the paused gate alive. Both persist; neither ruminates.

**P3. Defer discussion to the next boundary by default. [F1 transfer]**
Unless triage says redirect, the acknowledging sentence promises discussion *at the next gate exit*, not never ("parked as T-###, will surface at STRATEGY review"). High-workload mid-gate discussion is the measured-worst timing; boundaries are where resumption is cheapest. If the user pulls toward immediate discussion anyway (F3: principal initiative wins), comply and re-park the gate symmetrically.

**P4. Record both initiatives in the park line. [F5 transfer]**
The THOUGHT_LOG park entry gains two slots: task initiative (stays with gate G) and dialogue initiative (user's T-###, owed discussion at boundary B). Format: `paused G at <progress>, resume <micro-step>; T-### owed at <boundary>`. This makes the discuss-vs-protocol balance checkable at REVIEW instead of reconstructible from memory.

## 5. Not-to-Encode (explicit non-goals)

- **N1. Do not re-encode A4, raw-first, or the correction counterexample.** Park-verbatim mechanics, paused-gate statement, and correction-in-place stand as built; this brief adds triage, dose, and resume-line content only.
- **N2. Do not quote snippet-only numbers as findings.** ACM "reasonably high accuracy," diary-study effect directions, 84% XLNet precision, +2–4/+8–13pp initiative gains are single-study priors (LIKELY max), usable for sizing bets, never as design-rationale citations. Rationale cites F-directions only.
- **N3. Do not invert the notification literature blindly.** Suppression-by-default findings (S-survey) concern *system-initiated* alerts to users; a user's mid-run thought is *principal-initiated* and must never be suppressed — only deferred with a promised return boundary. Deferral without a named return is suppression wearing a nicer word.
- **N4. Do not merge T-003 into this fold.** The single-authoritative-design-file question draws on different literature (single-source-of-truth, documentation architecture) and different evidence (repo artifact audit, not interruption science). One sweep per thought (§6 G6).
- **N5. Do not demand discussion of every parked thought.** The nocuous-line principle applies: tangents that the boundary review finds moot stay parked with a one-line closure note. A handler that re-litigates every interruption is the rigidity T-002 complained about, rebuilt inside the fix.
