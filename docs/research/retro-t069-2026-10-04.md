## 3. Top Findings (with confidence + effect sizes)

**F1. FAIR as the pre-implementation method contract survives the re-sweep unchanged: no rival candidate surfaced. (CONFIRMED, carried)**
Prior extraction of Wilkinson et al. 2016 (F1–F4/A1–A2/I1–I3/R1–R1.3; "precede implementation… not, themselves, a standard"; incremental; non-data assets included; metadata-may-be-public pattern) stands — this sweep's FAIR hits (health-stewardship scoping protocol https://www.researchprotocols.org/2021/2/e22505; FAIR-Lite https://www.altex.org/index.php/altex/article/view/2939; robotics curation http://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00220v1) all *use* FAIR as the assumed contract rather than contesting it. No modification to data-governance-method §4.

**F2. The FAIR-implementation literature now documents its own failure mode: 13 frameworks, 36 parameters, technology-first, missing the what/why/how and the social aspects. (LIKELY, extractor-verified)**
Parametric review of openly available FAIR implementation frameworks: most offer step-by-step tool-deployment guides, "missing the critical aspects of explaining what, why, and how for the four foundational principles… giving less consideration to the social aspects"; authors call for people-first over technology-first frameworks (http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06268v1 — full abstract extracted via 127.0.0.1:8081, VERIFIED). This is the strongest new datum of the retro: independent convergence with data-governance-method Anti-Patterns #2 (Tool-in-the-method) and #3 (Open vocabulary), and with g8's "enumerated in schema, not free text" rule. Mechanism is directly encodable: method text states requirements + rationale per FAIR sub-principle, never tool names.

**F3. PROV-AGENT gives the method half its missing agentic-provenance citation: W3C PROV extended with prompts/responses/decisions via MCP + observability, evaluated edge/cloud/HPC. (LIKELY, extractor-verified primary)**
"Existing methods fail to capture and relate agent-centric metadata such as prompts, responses, and decisions with the broader workflow context"; contributions are the extended model, a near-real-time open-source capture system, and a cross-facility evaluation with provenance-query + reliability-analysis support (http://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02866v3 — abstract extracted via 127.0.0.1:8081, VERIFIED). Prior sweeps had NIDM/W3C-PROV as the provenance anchor but nothing agent-shaped; this fills exactly the "analysis provenance" slot data-governance-method §5/Gap-1 left open. Single paper — cap at LIKELY, do not quote eval numbers (none snippet-verified).

**F4. The OpenLineage producer/consumer split reconfirms as the architecture idiom: specs declare, runs emit, consumers index. (LIKELY, canonical page)**
Ecosystem page enumerates interoperating producers vs consumers around one lineage spec (https://openlineage.io/ecosystem/). Converges with g8 §3 without modification; no new producer/consumer pair surfaced this sweep. Strength stays LIKELY (project page, not peer-reviewed) — same as g8's basis.

**F5. Croissant reconfirms as the ML-metadata shape: schema.org/Dataset + resource/organisation/ML-semantics layers. (LIKELY, canonical page)**
Google Research announcement page returned as the top general hit (https://research.google/blog/croissant-a-metadata-format-for-ml-ready-datasets/). Converges with g8 §5; the RAI-vocabulary detail and validator/editor tooling claims are carried from g8, not re-verified here — do not upgrade them.

**F6. FAIR-for-workflows extends the contract to exactly the object the method governs: workflows as publishable, identified, metadata-bearing research objects. (UNVERIFIED, snippet-level)**
EOSC-Life Workflow Collaboratory pattern — persistent identifiers + machine-actionable metadata on workflows, WorkflowHub registry, LifeMonitor testing service, FAIR ecosystems for workflows drawn from FAIR data + software principles (http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15988v2). Directly supports data-governance-method §4's "analytical workflows are governed data too" line with a second citation — but snippet-only, so UNVERIFIED; adopt as design vocabulary, not evidence.

## 4. Design Principles for T-069 (encode these)

**P1. Spec owns decision rights; engine owns machinery — unchanged roof rule (data-governance-method §2 LIKELY).**
F1 (no rival to FAIR-as-contract) and F2 (tool-first frameworks fail on what/why/how) jointly strengthen the Khatri & Brown cut: *data principles, quality standards, metadata* in SPECIFICATION.md/STANDING_PRINCIPLES; retrieval/extraction/indexing in the engine session. No modification.

**P2. Pin the FAIR subset with rationale per sub-principle — UPGRADED from exhortation to guard.**
F2's critique (frameworks skip what/why/how + social aspects) makes "be FAIR" an encodable failure: the spec must name which sub-principles (F1–F4/A1–A2/I1–I3/R1–R1.3 subset), at which maturity level, *and why each matters to this protocol*. A FAIR line without rationale fails review.

**P3. Provenance quartet — unchanged (data-governance-method §5 LIKELY).**
No new trust-study datum arrived; F3 extends the *capture mechanism* (agent-centric metadata) but does not change the rule: source + provenance + quality signal + confidence on every load-bearing claim. F3's prompt/response/decision fields slot into the provenance leg.

**P4. Catalog once, reference everywhere; gate at entry — unchanged (data-governance-method §6 LIKELY).**
F4 (producer/consumer split) and F5 (Croissant layering) both reconfirm without modification. No new catalog or gate-placement datum.

**P5. [NEW] Govern agentic intermediates as PROV-AGENT-shaped records — prompts, responses, decisions linked to workflow context.**
F3's mechanism (extend W3C PROV with agent-centric metadata via MCP + observability; near-real-time capture) is implementable as the analysis-provenance record for ledger/synthesis artefacts: each derived artefact links input digests + method version/parameters (carried) *plus* the deciding prompt/response or rule that produced it. Cap: adopt the record shape, never claim the edge/cloud/HPC eval transfers to this protocol.

**P6. [NEW] Workflows are governed data — identifier + metadata on every reusable procedure.**
F6's vocabulary (workflows as first-class publishable objects with PIDs + machine-actionable metadata): LANDSCAPE strategies, synthesis templates, and ledger schemas get identifiers, versions, and metadata records like any dataset. UNVERIFIED basis — encode as convention, not as science-backed requirement.
