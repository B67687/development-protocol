# LANDSCAPE Research: Scales of Analysis — Philosophical→Macro→Meso→Micro, Whole-System Big→Small, Scale of Effect (THOUGHTS T-066 / T-067)

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Thoughts:** T-066 — analysis at different scales (philosophical→macro→meso→micro); T-067 — whole-system big→small sweep to find scale of effect
- **Core pattern:** A thinking agent must analyse at the scale where the effect lives, not the scale where the symptom shows: run the same problem down the ladder philosophical (paradigm/intent) → macro (goals/rules) → meso (feedback/information flows) → micro (parameters/constants), then sweep whole-system big→small to locate the scale of effect — because shallow fixes at the wrong scale are absorbed (policy resistance) while one deep move re-structures everything below it.
- **Method:** SearXNG self-hosted (127.0.0.1:8888, categories=science first, then general follow-ups), Trafilatura extractor sidecar (127.0.0.1:8081) probes on 5 targets (2 full-verified, 1 partial-redirect, 2 failed/empty), confidence labels per claim.
- **Confidence scheme:** CONFIRMED = replicated/meta-analytic or canonical primary source; LIKELY = single-study or strong convergent theory; UNVERIFIED = plausible, needs verification.

## 1. Problem Statement

T-066 observes a scale-confusion failure mode: analysis conducted entirely at one altitude — pure philosophy with no mechanism, or pure micro-tweaking with no intent — so the diagnosis names a scale the intervention cannot reach. T-067 adds the search procedure: sweep the whole system big→small until the scale of effect reveals itself (the level at which a change propagates rather than dissipates). The research question: what does the literature say about (a) hierarchy theory's account of levels, constraints, and cross-scale causation (what constrains what, downward vs upward), (b) Meadows' leverage-points ladder as a ready-made philosophical→micro scale (paradigm down to constants), (c) scale-of-effect evidence from ecology (local vs landscape vs macro, panarchy, cross-scale morphology), and (d) socio-technical scale (why architectural/governance fixes outlast local/component fixes) — and what must therefore be encoded so an agent analyses at every scale and intervenes at the scale of effect?

## 3. Top Findings (with confidence + effect sizes)

**F1. The 12 leverage points are already a philosophical→micro scale — and the ordering is the T-066 ladder. (CONFIRMED as canonical hierarchy; LIKELY as effectiveness ordering, extractor-verified primary)**

Meadows' canonical essay (https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/, extractor-verified: leverage lore, Forrester "pushing in the wrong direction" anecdote, buffers discussion) orders places to intervene from shallow to deep: 12 constants/parameters/numbers → 11 buffer sizes → 10 material stocks/flows structure → 9 delays → 8 balancing-loop strength → 7 reinforcing-loop strength → 6 information-flow structure → 5 rules (incentives/punishments/constraints) → 4 self-organisation power → 3 system goals → 2 paradigm/mindset → 1 transcending paradigms. Book-chapter twin records (https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/9781136551406/chapters/10.4324/9781849773386-15; https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/9781315479057/chapters/10.4324/9781315479057-39, snippet). The sustainability აღ synthesis (https://doi.org/10.1002/pan3.13, snippet): four advantages — bridges causal+teleological explanation, names deep points, examines shallow↔deep interaction, works as boundary object. T-066 corollary: adopt Meadows' ladder verbatim as the analysis scale — philosophical = points 1–2 (paradigm), macro = 3–5 (goals, self-organisation, rules), meso = 6–7 (information, feedback gains), micro = 8–12 (delays, stocks, buffers, parameters). No scale invented; the ladder is inherited.

**F2. Real intervention portfolios cluster at the shallow end — the precise failure T-066/T-067 exist to prevent. (LIKELY, convergent single-studies, snippet-only)**

Sports-systems audit of 80 highly-cited papers across 8 journals (https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2024.2352681, snippet): sports science/nutrition/biomechanics/medicine concentrate at the shallow end; only management/policy-law reach the deep end; explanation — practitioner "think fast" pressure + weak engagement with systems/methods experts. SDG governance architecture (https://doi.org/10.32388/ozjpoz; twin https://www.qeios.com/read/OZJPOZ; v2 https://doi.org/10.32388/ozjpoz.2, snippet): 169 targets + indicators + voluntary reviews = aspirational parameters + weakly coupled information flows; Goal 8 reinscribes growth as system goal; trade-offs/burden-shifting/selective progress read as policy resistance. Transparency-as-leverage cases Ukraine/Romania/Iceland (https://doi.org/10.3390/su142416801, snippet): transparency alone misplaced without accountability + standards — information-flow fix needs rule-level coupling. T-066 corollary: the agent's default failure mode is empirically the modal failure mode — shallow clustering under time pressure. The four-scale analysis is a forcing function against it.

**F3. Higher levels constrain lower ones; effects cascade downward asymmetrically — the licence for big→small search. (LIKELY theory structure; macroevolution asymmetry CONFIRMED as stated regularity, snippet-only)**

Hierarchy-theory exposition for complex systems (https://hess.copernicus.org/preprints/6/2931/2009/, extractor partial — redirect stub only, abstract via snippet): define levels of analysis, theory predicts, narrative explains; hierarchical treatment tames modelling complexity. Scale-and-hierarchy in macroevolution (https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1475-4983.2006.00615.x, snippet): three stated regularities — (1) upward+downward causation linked, emergent focal properties never fully independent; (2) hierarchical effects asymmetrical — dynamics at a focal level need not propagate upward but always cascade downward; (3) rates generally faster at lower levels. Hierarchy book chapters (https://academic.oup.com/chicago-scholarship-online/book/30712/chapter/261270464; https://academic.oup.com/chicago-scholarship-online/book/30712/chapter/261271066, snippet): identifying the scale in community ecology; scale as investigative tool. T-067 corollary: sweep big→small because causation is asymmetric — a goal/rule change cascades to every parameter below it, while a parameter tweak rarely propagates up. Search order follows causal privilege, not convenience.

**F4. Scale of effect is empirically real and mismatched scales explain null results — local fixes in landscape-scale systems mitigate but do not restore. (LIKELY, single-experiment + convergent review, snippet-only)**

Panarchy liming experiment 1998–2019 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2022.116875, snippet): multiscale temporal structure found for littoral invertebrates; liming proxy (Ca:Mg) correlated with only a few fluctuation frequencies; effect diluted; management fails to enforce cross-scale interactions — liming mitigates rather than restores. Panarchy theory record (http://link.springer.com/10.1007/s10021-013-9744-2, snippet) + 20-year review of 2,177 documents (http://arxiv.org/abs/2012.14312v1, snippet): adaptive cycle drew most attention; empirical grounding of the metaphor remains the open challenge. Convergent: macro→local diversity theory (https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-2699.2001.00563.x, snippet) — local mechanisms dominate locally, macro richness climatically determined, local communities non-equilibrial yet macro pattern persists; top-down global-to-local modelling advocated. Landscape-ecology scaling limits (https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2019.00293, snippet): three intrinsic limits — coarse-graining (fine→large aggregation bias), middle-number problem (too few for averaging, too many for tractability), non-stationarity (parameters valid in one regime fail in the next). Cross-scale morphology (https://doi.org/10.1002/9780470057339.vac061.pub2; twin https://doi.org/10.1002/9780470057339.vac061, snippet): departures from universal scaling laws mark self-organised living↔physical interaction over narrow scale ranges. Conservation planning (http://link.springer.com/10.1007/s10980-010-9447-4, snippet): regional-vs-local trade-offs and omissions. Cross-scale interactions working paper (https://refubium.fu-berlin.de/handle/fub188/18927, snippet): no single solution across scales; learn across understandings, negotiate across viewpoints. T-067 corollary: the agent's "scale of effect" question has a standing empirical answer — test whether the intervention enforces cross-scale interaction; if it correlates with only a few of the system's fluctuation frequencies, it is mitigation, and the spec must say so.

**F5. Architectural (governance/rule) fixes move system-level metrics by multiples; component fixes do not — socio-technical scale, simulated. (LIKELY direction; single-simulation magnitudes UNVERIFIED beyond snippet, extractor-verified intro)**

SoS governance proof-of-concept, Petri-Net simulation (https://doi.org/10.3390/systems13121113, extractor-verified intro: fragmentation framing, HL7/FHIR-without-context + contract-without-runtime diagnosis): CIC adaptive-interface governance cut fragmentation 0.8077→0.1538 and raised successful interactions 68→339 over 400 steps (snippet numbers) — a rule/interface-level intervention moving system metrics ~5×. Backdrop: socio-technical systems thinking record (https://scienceopen.com/hosted-document?doi=10.54120%2Fjost.000012, snippet; extractor empty), urban-water review (https://doi.org/10.1080/10643389.2022.2131338, snippet: root causes + enabling environment over component upgrades), agentic-AI socio-technical analysis (http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06064v1, snippet: architectural choices in perception/cognition/planning/memory co-produce accountability/transparency/safety outcomes), transport cyber-attack detection via physical+social redundancy (http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11422v1, snippet). Systems-thinking textbook/handbook records (https://doi.org/10.29173/oer403i4dh7; https://link.springer.com/10.1007/978-3-030-46054-9_36-2; https://link.springer.com/10.1007/978-3-030-46054-9_36-3, snippet). T-067 corollary: when the sweep finds the effect at macro (rules/interfaces/goals), a local patch is not a cheaper version of the fix — it is a different, lower-leverage intervention. Spec the architectural fix; list the local patch as mitigation with its non-propagation noted.

## 4. Design Principles for T-066/T-067 (encode these)

**P1. Analyse every problem at all four scales on Meadows' ladder, and record the scale of effect explicitly (F1 + F3).**
One row per scale — philosophical (paradigm/intent, points 1–2), macro (goals/rules/self-organisation, 3–5), meso (information/flows/feedback, 6–7), micro (delays/stocks/buffers/parameters, 8–12) — each naming candidate cause, candidate intervention, and expected propagation. The row where change propagates downward through the rows below is the scale of effect; write it in the spec as one sentence. No analysis with fewer than four rows ships.

**P2. Sweep big→small, and stop downward at the first scale that propagates — never start at micro (F3 + F4).**
Causal privilege sets search order (F3 asymmetry): test philosophical first, then macro, meso, micro. Promote the first scale whose intervention enforces cross-scale interaction (F4 test); demote everything below it to mitigation. A micro-first investigation is a process violation even when it accidentally works.

**P3. Prefer the architectural fix at the scale of effect; label local fixes as mitigation with their non-propagation noted (F5 + F2).**
When the scale of effect is macro or above, spec the rule/interface/goal change (F5: governance-level moves shift system metrics by multiples), and record any accompanying local patch as mitigation-only with the frequencies it does not move (F4). Shallow clustering under time pressure (F2) is the named temptation; the spec review checks the scale-of-effect sentence against it.

## 5. Not-to-Encode (explicit non-goals)

- **N1. Do not encode single-scale analysis.** Any diagnosis or fix naming only one scale is rejected at review, however elegant (F1's ladder is the minimum; F4 shows single-scale fixes dilute).
- **N2. Do not cite Meadows, panarchy, or hierarchy theory as evidence.** Doctrines and review records (F1–F3 anchors) are shorthands, never justifications. Justifications cite propagation tests and scale-of-effect observations.
- **N3. Do not relabel mitigation as restoration.** F4's liming verdict forbids it: an intervention correlating with a few frequencies that leaves cross-scale interaction unenforced is mitigation, and the spec must use that word.
- **N4. Do not intervene deep without naming the juncture.** S1's critical-junction finding: deep leverage opens at particular times; a paradigm/goal intervention with no timing analysis is a wish, not a plan.
- **N5. Do not aggregate across scales naively.** F4's three limits (coarse-graining bias, middle-number intractability, non-stationarity) forbid treating fine-scale evidence as directly summable to landscape claims without a stated scaling law.
