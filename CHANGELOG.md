## [Unreleased]
- Prompt standards (`docs/PROMPT_STANDARDS.md`, new): the step files are prompt text, so the protocol now follows the instruction-following literature instead of our intuition. Two registers in one file — operational text (gates, imperatives, prohibitions, checklists) is bound by eight rules; explanatory text (rationale, provenance, examples, history) stays free and may be as long as it is useful. The eight: re-inject at the point of use; cap and retire co-active constraints; prefer unconditional checklists over chained conditions; state prohibitions positively with an enforcement check on critical ones; keep load-bearing rules at file edges; make deliberation templates opt-in and A/B them; keep live context small (one phase file, re-inject after compaction); instrument adherence with rule-checked spot checks, never a model judge. Three of the eight are lint-able and are enforced as lint Rule 10 (negation density, checkbox ratchet, research index); the other five stay manual, and the standard says so. Eleven load-bearing prohibitions rewritten in `EXECUTOR`, `RULES` and `STANDARDS` (for example 'never decide for the user' → 'leave the decision with the user', 'MUST NOT refactor code they were not asked to modify' → 'refactor only the code they were asked to modify'); the negative forms that carry meaning (scope exclusions, per-phase 'Not allowed' lists, NO-GOS) are exempt. Evidence base: `docs/research/prompt-engineering-science.md` (12 findings with confidence labels, 9 open gaps) plus a new `docs/research/INDEX.md`, whose row Rule 10c enforces. `RULES.md` also gains § Research is saved when it is produced. Lint 12/12 PASS.
- **Domain norms (F-020):** a general standard is not enough to make work good, so every run now declares its domain and 2-5 domain craft norms (each checkable, each tagged `known` / `researched` / `asked`), LANDSCAPE searches for those norms when the domain is unfamiliar, and REVIEW row 4.11 verifies the artifact against them. New `docs/HOUSE_STANDARD.md` § Domain instantiation; declaration line in `steps/AMBITION.md` round 5; search target in `steps/LANDSCAPE.md` Step 2. Grounded in SC2001, where the structure held and the missing thing was a domain norm (a reported optimum survives a repeat). Lint 12/12 PASS.
- **The internal loop, layered adversarial descent (T-026 + T-032).** The FINISH gate is now a bounded descent instead of a single checklist pass: L1 structure, L2 behavior, L3 craft, L4 surface, each with a fresh-eyed adversary and its own source of truth, gated so a layer is clean before the next descent. Stop rule: two consecutive descents with no new class of problem (budget 3, 5 for Deep) - unbounded *iterate until satisfied* is how a one-week project becomes a three-month one. Exit artifact: the handover residue, only items that are unknowable-from-evidence AND cheap for the human, each with a proposed default, never a structural item; REVIEW row 4.12 fails a run that hands over one. The existing adversarial review agent is the outside pass on the loop result. Wired: EXECUTOR section The internal loop, REVIEW (outside-pass note + row 4.12), STANDING_PRINCIPLES (the 3-pass rule at artifact scale), HOUSE_STANDARD cross-ref, FEATURES F-021. Lint 12/12 PASS.
- **Spec architecture, Stage 1 (T-035/036/037):** `docs/RESEARCH_BASIS.md` justifies each mechanism (`mechanism | claim | research basis with effect size | SWE practice it maps to | falsifier`, 13 rows including the SC2001 and FC-2026-004/005 in-house findings); a context-declaration rule (term · domain · one-line definition · prerequisites · contrast · why here) sits beside the readings gate in `steps/EXTRACTION.md`, cross-referenced from `steps/INBOX.md` and `steps/RULES.md`; `steps/REVIEW.md` Phase 4 gains row 4.13 (declaration completeness). Stage 2 (manifest + lint Rule 11) follows in its own commit. Option B chosen over A/C in `.omo/plans/spec-architecture.md`.
- **Protocol document architecture (T-035/036/037/038, option B Stage 2)** — `docs/PROTOCOL_MODEL.md` becomes the structural manifest: its states table gains altitude (P1-P4), entry artifact and exit artifact for all 15 states, a supporting-files line names the six non-state step files, and a justification-index line links `docs/RESEARCH_BASIS.md`. The altitude spine is canonicalised: `steps/QUICKSTART.md` defines it, and the identical literal now appears in `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `PROTOCOL_MODEL.md` and `CONSTITUTION.md`. New lint **Rule 11** — 11a every `steps/*.md` appears in the manifest, 11b the spine literal agrees across the four files and the AGENTS tree lists every step, 11c `RESEARCH_BASIS.md` has >=8 complete rows and is linked from the manifest. - **Research: how thinking feels like progress (T-040/T-042).** A literature sweep on why planning gates read as a chore, recorded at `docs/research/human-thinking-preferences-2026-09-24.md` and indexed. What it licenses: satisfy autonomy/competence/relatedness in every gate; keep each gate inside the challenge-skill channel with one-sentence goals and feedback closed inside the gate; close every gate visibly (small wins) while previewing the next one unstarted; cut extraneous form-load before touching standards; keep generative struggle and pair it with consolidation. What it rules out: points, badges, leaderboards, streaks, contingent rewards, engagement-maximising patterns, and sanding away all difficulty. Counterweight recorded honestly: desirable difficulties feel bad by design, so some chore-feeling is the learning mechanism -- the target is form-friction, never thought-friction. Nothing built from it yet: T-040's build gate stays closed until a live run shows which gate actually feels like a chore.

- Philosophy stated at the top of the protocol (`docs/CONSTITUTION.md` § The philosophy): fix in planning what can be fixed in planning, and prototyping is planning. - House standard and the taste boundary (`docs/HOUSE_STANDARD.md`, new): the request _"you should have your own objective taste, as long as the foundation you set is good"_ is right in substance and wrong in one word. Taste is split into four layers — T0 house standard (consistency, fitness for the stated purpose, legibility to the declared reader, defensibility of claims, freedom from self-serving embellishment), owned by the agent and applied always; T1 objective, owned by the agent; T2 evidenced taste (a preference the user demonstrated in their own artifacts or ratified this run), applied with each inference flagged; T3 novel taste, asked with a proposed default and one line on what changes if the answer differs. Every finished artifact declares the house standard it was built to, so a default stays visible as a default instead of passing as a requirement or as the user's own past preference; enforced as `REVIEW.md` row 4.10, with pointers from CONSTITUTION "What follows" item 3, the `RULES.md` objectivity duty, `STANDARDS.md` §14, and `EXECUTOR.md` § Production Quality Requirements. The three existing statements of the taste decision keep their force for novel taste only. Grounded by `docs/research/ai-taste-judgment.md` (taste behaves as a capability limit with scaffolding gains plateauing; homogenisation persists; no evidence of taste transfer from world models; no one-year fix claimed) plus a row in `docs/research/INDEX.md`. Lint 12/12 PASS.

- Craft-primary ordering (`docs/HOUSE_STANDARD.md` § Craft before gates, new): two levels, not equal. The foundation is how the thing is made — for code, names a reader can follow, structure that shows the seams, no code that merely passes; for prose, rhythm and concision; for a measurement, the method stated and the number holding up under a repeat. No tool produces that level. The deterministic checks — linters, type checkers, tests, the polish checklist, the review rows — are the floor that follows, and they cannot make anything good. **Instrumentation is a floor, never a ceiling, and a green checklist is never a claim of quality**; no tool is removed and no gate is relaxed, because the claim is about significance, not necessity. The order is named where it bites (`EXECUTOR.md` § FINISH gate, `REVIEW.md` Phase 4) and registered as FEATURES F-019. Grounded in the expert-lens finding that our machinery was denser at the mechanical level than at the craft level. Lint 12/12 PASS.
